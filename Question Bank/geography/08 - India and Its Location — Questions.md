@qgeo.india_and_its_location.q1 difficulty=recall subject=geography chapter=8
In terms of land area, India is the _____ largest country in the world and occupies roughly _____ of the world's land area.
- A) 5th; 4.2%
- B) 7th; 2.4%
- C) 6th; 3.5%
- D) 8th; 2.0%
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

The chapter states: "In terms of area: 7th (India occupies 2.4% of the world's area)."

**Trap:** Candidates confuse India's area rank (7th) with its population rank (1st) and pick 5th or 6th; the 2.4% figure is also specific and not interchangeable with other percentages.

</details>

@qgeo.india_and_its_location.q4 difficulty=recall subject=geography chapter=8
Which Union Territory contains India's southernmost extreme point, Indira Point / Pygmalion Point?
- A) Lakshadweep
- B) Puducherry
- C) Andaman & Nicobar Islands
- D) Daman & Diu
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C**

The extreme-points table lists the Southernmost in the Andaman & Nicobar chain as Indira Point / Pygmalion Point; this is in the Andaman & Nicobar Islands UT. (Note: on the mainland, the southernmost point is Kanyakumari in Tamil Nadu, and the easternmost mainland point is Kibithu in Arunachal Pradesh.)

**Trap:** Candidates confuse the easternmost mainland point (Kibithu, Arunachal Pradesh) with the southernmost island point (Indira Point, A&N). The question asks specifically for the southernmost extreme that includes Indira Point — that is in the A&N Islands.

**See also:** [[../../geography/08 - India and Its Location#Extreme Points in India]]

@qgeo.india_and_its_location.q5 difficulty=apply subject=geography chapter=8
A traveler leaves Gujarat (longitude ~68°7′E) by train for Arunachal Pradesh (longitude ~97°25′E). Roughly how much local solar time separates the two states, ignoring official time zones?
- A) About 30 minutes
- B) About 1 hour
- C) About 2 hours
- D) About 3 hours
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C**

The chapter explicitly notes: "The difference of time between Gujarat and Arunachal Pradesh is 2 hours." This arises because Earth rotates 15° per hour and the longitudinal difference is about 29°18′ (~29.3°), close to 2 hours of solar time.

**Trap:** Candidates may compute 1° = 4 minutes and divide 29° by 4 to get ~7 hours — a unit mistake; 1 hour corresponds to 15°, not 1°. Or they may simply remember that "India follows IST" and pick 0 hours, missing the chapter's specific 2-hour figure.

**See also:** [[../../geography/08 - India and Its Location#Indian Standard Time]]
</details>

@qgeo.india_and_its_location.q8 difficulty=apply subject=geography chapter=8
The boundary line that separates India from Pakistan, drawn by Sir Cyril Radcliffe, is called the:
- A) McMahon Line
- B) Radcliffe Line
- C) Durand Line
- D) Line of Control
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

The chapter states: "The Radcliffe Line (Sir Cyril Radcliffe drew the line) is the boundary that separates India and Pakistan." The Line of Control (LoC) is a separate military demarcation within the disputed J&K region, the McMahon Line is the India-China eastern boundary, and the Durand Line is the India-Afghanistan boundary.

**Trap:** All four "lines" get mixed up; only the Radcliffe Line is the India-Pakistan boundary proper. The LoC is a military line within that boundary.

**See also:** [[../../geography/08 - India and Its Location#India and Pakistan Border Shared By]]
</details>

@qgeo.india_and_its_location.q9 difficulty=apply subject=geography chapter=8
Under UNCLOS, India's Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) extends up to how many nautical miles from the coast?
- A) 12 nm
- B) 24 nm
- C) 200 nm
- D) 500 nm
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C**

The chapter states: "India's territorial limit extend up to 12 nm (12 nm = 22 km) from the coast. Contiguous Zone ... extends up to 24 nm. Exclusive Economic Zone: It is a maritime area that extends up to 200 nm from a coastal nation's territorial sea."

**Trap:** Candidates confuse 12 nm (territorial sea), 24 nm (contiguous zone), and 200 nm (EEZ). All three numbers appear in the same section.

**See also:** [[../../geography/08 - India and Its Location#Territorial Limit of India]]
</details>

@qgeo.india_and_its_location.q12 difficulty=tricky subject=geography chapter=8
A border-guarding force tasked specifically with India's frontier with China along the Indo-Tibetan region is the:
- A) Border Security Force (BSF)

BSF guards Pakistan and Bangladesh, SSB guards Nepal and Bhutan, and Assam Rifles operates mainly in the northeast under Ministry of Defence control.

**Trap:** Multiple CAPFs operate on different frontiers; only ITBP is specifically tasked with the India-China border.
