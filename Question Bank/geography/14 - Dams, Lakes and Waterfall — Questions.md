# Dams, Lakes and Waterfall — Questions

@qgeo.dams_lakes_waterfall.q1 difficulty=recall subject=geography chapter=14
Indias first river-valley project (1948) was based on the Tennessee River Project (USA). Which river system was it built on?

- A) Mahanadi
- B) Damodar
- C) Kosi
- D) Sutlej
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

The chapter states: "Damodar Valley Project — It is Indias first river valley project (1948) based on the Tennessee River Project (USA)." It includes four main dams (Maithon, Tilaiya, Panchet, Konar) all in Jharkhand.

**Trap:** Candidates confuse the Damodar Valley Project (1948, India first) with the Bhakra Nangal Project (Sutlej, First Five-Year Plan). Both are famous river-valley projects but on different rivers and from different eras.

**See also:** [[../../geography/14 - Dams, Lakes and Waterfall#MAJOR DAMS/PROJECTS]]
</details>

@qgeo.dams_lakes_waterfall.q2 difficulty=recall subject=geography chapter=14
The Tehri Dam is the highest dam of India. It is built on which river?

- A) Alaknanda
- B) Bhagirathi
- C) Mandakini
- D) Ganga
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

The chapter states: "Tehri Dam — Built on: Bhagirathi river (Uttarakhand). It is the highest dam of India (Height: 261 m)."

**Trap:** Bhagirathi and Alaknanda both form the Ganga at Devprayag, but the Tehri Dam is on the Bhagirathi tributary specifically, not the Alaknanda or the main Ganga.

**See also:** [[../../geography/14 - Dams, Lakes and Waterfall#MAJOR DAMS/PROJECTS]]
</details>

@qgeo.dams_lakes_waterfall.q3 difficulty=recall subject=geography chapter=14
Which dam is the longest earthen dam in the world and the longest dam in India?

- A) Bhakra Dam
- B) Tehri Dam
- C) Hirakud Dam
- D) Nagarjuna Sagar Dam
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C**

The chapter states: "Hirakud Dam — Built on: Mahanadi river (Sambalpur district of Odisha). It is the longest earthen dam in the world (the main dam is 4.8 km long and the dam and dykes together measure 25.8 km)."

**Trap:** Candidates confuse "highest dam" (Tehri, 261 m on Bhagirathi) with "longest dam" (Hirakud on Mahanadi). Bhakra Nangal is the largest dam in terms of *area*, and forms Gobind Sagar Lake.

**See also:** [[../../geography/14 - Dams, Lakes and Waterfall#MAJOR DAMS/PROJECTS]]
</details>

@qgeo.dams_lakes_waterfall.q4 difficulty=recall subject=geography chapter=14
The largest freshwater lake in India (formed due to tectonic activity) is:

- A) Dal Lake
- B) Chilika Lake
- C) Wular Lake
- D) Loktak Lake
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C**

The chapter states: "Largest freshwater lake in India: Wular Lake (it was formed as a result of tectonic activity)" — located in Jammu and Kashmir.

**Trap:** Candidates confuse Wular (freshwater, tectonic) with Chilika (largest *brackish* water lake, Odisha) and Loktak (worlds only *floating* lake, Manipur).

@qgeo.dams_lakes_waterfall.q5 difficulty=apply subject=geography chapter=14
A student is told: "This dam is built on the Sutlej river, was constructed during the First Five-Year Plan, is Indias highest gravity dam, and forms Gobind Sagar Lake." The dam being described is:

- A) Hirakud Dam
- B) Bhakra Nangal Project
- C) Pong Dam
- D) Thein (Ranjit Sagar) Dam
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

The chapter states: "Bhakra Nangal Project — Built on: Sutlej river (during the First Five-Year Plan). It is Indias highest Gravity Dam and the largest dam in terms of area. It consists of two dams: Bhakra Dam (located in Himachal Pradesh, which also forms Gobind Sagar Lake) and Nangal Dam (located in Punjab)."

**Trap:** Pong Dam is also on the Beas (not Sutlej). Thein/Ranjit Sagar is on the Ravi in Punjab. Hirakud is on the Mahanadi. The trick is that the *only* Sutlej dam described in this much detail is Bhakra Nangal.

**See also:** [[../../geography/14 - Dams, Lakes and Waterfall#MAJOR DAMS/PROJECTS]]
</details>

@qgeo.dams_lakes_waterfall.q6 difficulty=apply subject=geography chapter=14
Match each dam with the river on which it is built:

- Dam 1: Sardar Sarovar
- Dam 2: Pong Dam
- Dam 3: Thein Dam (Ranjit Sagar)
- Dam 4: Idukki Dam

Rivers: (a) Beas, (b) Sutlej, (c) Narmada, (d) Periyar.

Which option correctly maps all four?

- A) 1-c, 2-a, 3-b, 4-d
- B) 1-c, 2-b, 3-a, 4-d
- C) 1-a, 2-c, 3-d, 4-b
- D) 1-d, 2-a, 3-b, 4-c
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

The chapter lists: Sardar Sarovar Dam: Narmada river (Gujarat). Pong Dam: Beas river (Himachal Pradesh). Pong is in Kangra district and is also known as Maharana Pratap Sagar Dam. Thein Dam (Ranjit Sagar Dam): Ravi river (Punjab). Idukki Dam: not the river directly in the table but the chapter places it in Kerala; Periyar is the lifeline of Kerala river on which the Mullaperiyar Dam sits (Idukki is on the Periyar too).

**Trap:** The Beas and Sutlej pair is the trap — Pong Dam is on the Beas, not the Sutlej (Bhakra Nangal is on the Sutlej). The Ravi vs Beas swap is a common confusion in Punjab/Himachal dam questions.

**See also:** [[../../geography/14 - Dams, Lakes and Waterfall#STATE-WISE DAMS]]
</details>

@qgeo.dams_lakes_waterfall.q7 difficulty=apply subject=geography chapter=14
The Farakka Dam was built with what primary purpose?

- A) Hydroelectric power generation for West Bengal
- B) Providing water to the Hugli river
- C) Irrigation of the Kaveri delta
- D) Flood control on the Mahanadi
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

The chapter states: "Farakka Dam — Built on: Ganga river (West Bengal). This dam was built to provide water to Hugli river."

**Trap:** A and D are invented purposes. C is wrong — Farakka is on the Ganga, not the Kaveri (Kaveri delta irrigation is the focus of the Grand Anicut / Mettur Dam). The Hugli is a distributary of the Ganga; the dam helps flush sediment and maintain navigability of the Kolkata port.

**See also:** [[../../geography/14 - Dams, Lakes and Waterfall#MAJOR DAMS/PROJECTS]]
</details>

@qgeo.dams_lakes_waterfall.q8 difficulty=apply subject=geography chapter=14
The worlds only floating lake is in India. It is called:

- A) Dal Lake (Jammu and Kashmir)
- B) Loktak Lake (Manipur)
- C) Chilika Lake (Odisha)
- D) Vembanad Lake (Kerala)
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

The chapter states: "Worlds only floating lake: Loktak Lake (it is famous for phumdis floating over it. Keibul Lamjao National Park (worlds only floating National Park) is located here)."

**Trap:** Vembanad is the *longest* lake in India (Kerala), not the floating one. Dal is the "Jewel of Srinagar." Chilika is the largest *brackish* water lake.

**See also:** [[../../geography/14 - Dams, Lakes and Waterfall#Manipur]]
</details>

@qgeo.dams_lakes_waterfall.q9 difficulty=apply subject=geography chapter=14
The Sambhar lake (Rajasthan) is famous as:

- A) The largest freshwater lake in India
- B) The largest inland saltwater lake in India
- C) The largest brackish water lake in India
- D) The worlds only floating lake
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

The chapter states: "Largest inland saltwater lake: Sambhar lake (Chilika is a coastal lake)." Sambhar is in Rajasthan (near Jaipur/Ajmer).

**Trap:** C is the trap — Chilika is the largest *brackish* water lake in India (and the first Ramsar site, 1981). Candidates confuse inland-salt (Sambhar) with coastal-brackish (Chilika). A and D describe Wular and Loktak respectively.

**See also:** [[../../geography/14 - Dams, Lakes and Waterfall#Rajasthan]]
</details>

@qgeo.dams_lakes_waterfall.q10 difficulty=tricky subject=geography chapter=14
Which of the following is the correct pairing of Indian waterfalls with their rivers and states?

- A) Kunchikal falls (highest in India, 455 m) — Varahi river — Karnataka
- B) Jog Falls (highest in India) — Sharavati river — Karnataka
- C) Dudh Sagar (highest in India) — Mandovi river — Goa
- D) Athirappilly (highest in India) — Periyar river — Kerala
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

The chapter states: "Kunchikal (highest waterfall: 455 m) | Varahi | Karnataka (Shimoga)." Jog Falls is the 2nd highest (also on Sharavati, Karnataka). Dudh Sagar is on the Mandovi (Goa) but is not the highest. Athirappilly is on the Chalakudy river (Kerala), not Periyar.

**Trap:** Jog Falls is the most famous and is often misremembered as the *highest*. The chapter is explicit: Kunchikal is #1, Jog is #2.

**See also:** [[../../geography/14 - Dams, Lakes and Waterfall#Some Important Waterfalls of India]]
</details>

@qgeo.dams_lakes_waterfall.q11 difficulty=tricky subject=geography chapter=14
Consider the following about Lonar Lake:
(1) Lonar Lake is in the Buldhana district of Maharashtra and was created by a meteorite impact.
(2) Lonar Lake is a Ramsar site famous as a crater lake and is the only crater lake in India formed by volcanic activity.

Which of the following is correct?

- A) Both statements are true
- B) Only statement 1 is true; statement 2 is false (the crater was formed by meteorite impact, not volcanic activity)
- C) Only statement 2 is true; statement 1 is false
- D) Both statements are false
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

Statement 1 is true: "Lonar lake (also called crater lake (created due to meteorite impact) in Buldhana district)" — Maharashtra. Statement 2 is false because it claims the crater was formed by *volcanic* activity. The chapter explicitly says "meteorite impact," not volcanic. Lonar is a meteorite crater lake, not a volcanic one.

**Trap:** Candidates confuse Lonar with other crater lakes worldwide that are volcanic. The chapter is unambiguous: Lonar = meteorite impact.

**See also:** [[../../geography/14 - Dams, Lakes and Waterfall#Maharashtra]]
</details>

@qgeo.dams_lakes_waterfall.q12 difficulty=tricky subject=geography chapter=14
Assertion (A): The Bhakra Nangal Project consists of two dams — Bhakra Dam in Himachal Pradesh (forming Gobind Sagar Lake) and Nangal Dam in Punjab — and is built on the Sutlej river.
Reason (R): The project was constructed during the First Five-Year Plan and is Indias highest gravity dam and the largest dam in terms of area.
Which of the following is correct?

- A) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
- B) Both A and R are true, but R is NOT the correct explanation of A
- C) A is true but R is false
- D) A is false but R is false
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

Both A and R are factually correct per the chapter. A describes the structural composition of the Bhakra Nangal Project (Bhakra + Nangal, Sutlej, H.P./Punjab). R adds additional facts (First Five-Year Plan, highest gravity dam, largest by area). However, R describes *additional* facts about the same project — it does not *explain* why A is true (i.e. R does not explain why there are two dams or why one is in H.P. and the other in Punjab). They are both true but R is not the correct explanation of A.

**Trap:** A is the obvious "looks-right" pick because both A and R are factually true. The test is whether R *explains* A — it merely adds more true facts, not a causal explanation.

**See also:** [[../../geography/14 - Dams, Lakes and Waterfall#MAJOR DAMS/PROJECTS]]
</details>
