# World Map — Questions

A 12-question MCQ set on world geography — continents, straits, seas, deserts, mountain ranges, and cities on rivers. Calibrated to SSC CGL Tier-I standard.

@qgeo.world_map.q1 difficulty=recall subject=geography chapter=19
Which is the largest continent of the world by area?

- A) Africa
- B) North America
- C) Asia
- D) Europe
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C**

The chapter states: "Top (Area + Population wise): Asia. 2nd (Area + Population wise): Africa." Asia is the largest in both area and population.

**Trap:** A is #2 in both metrics. Candidates confuse 1st and 2nd.

**See also:** [[../../geography/19 - World Map#Continents]]
</details>

@qgeo.world_map.q2 difficulty=recall subject=geography chapter=19
The Strait of Malacca connects which two water bodies?

- A) Bay of Bengal and Arabian Sea
- B) South China Sea (Pacific Ocean) and Andaman Sea (Indian Ocean)
- C) Red Sea and Gulf of Aden
- D) Persian Gulf and Gulf of Oman
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

The chapter states: "Strait of Malacca — It connects South China Sea (part of Pacific Ocean) and Andaman Sea (part of Indian Ocean) and separates Malaysia and Indonesia."

**Trap:** A is not a strait (Bay of Bengal and Arabian Sea meet at the southern tip of India). C is the Bab-al-Mandab. D is the Strait of Hormuz.

**See also:** [[../../geography/19 - World Map#Strait of Malacca]]
</details>

@qgeo.world_map.q3 difficulty=recall subject=geography chapter=19
Which African river is the longest river in the world?

- A) Congo
- B) Niger
- C) Nile
- D) Limpopo
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C**

The chapter states: "Nile River — It is the longest river of the world. It is formed from Blue Nile (origin: Lake Tana in Ethiopia) and White Nile (origin: Lake Victoria of Tanzania). The Nile river empties at Mediterranean Sea."

**Trap:** Congo and Limpopo are African rivers but not the longest. Niger is also major but not the longest.

**See also:** [[../../geography/19 - World Map#River]]
</details>

@qgeo.world_map.q4 difficulty=recall subject=geography chapter=19
The Andes Mountain Range is best described as:

- A) The longest chain of mountains on land, a young fold mountain range running along western South America
- B) An old fold mountain range in central Asia
- C) A volcanic island arc in the western Pacific
- D) A block mountain range in central Africa
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

The chapter states: "Andes Mountain Range — It is the longest chain of mountain range on land that runs along the western coast of South America. It is a young fold mountain range."

**Trap:** B describes the Urals (or the Appalachians in North America). C is the Ring of Fire / Japan. D is the Ethiopian Highlands / Atlas.

**See also:** [[../../geography/19 - World Map#Andes Mountain Range]]
</details>

@qgeo.world_map.q5 difficulty=apply subject=geography chapter=19
Match each strait with the water bodies it connects:

- Strait 1: Bab-al-Mandab
- Strait 2: Bosphorus
- Strait 3: Palk Strait
- Strait 4: Bering Strait

Connections: (a) Black Sea and Sea of Marmara, (b) Red Sea and Gulf of Aden, (c) Bay of Bengal and Gulf of Mannar (separates India and Sri Lanka), (d) Arctic Ocean and Pacific Ocean (separates Asia and North America).

Which option correctly pairs all four?

- A) 1-b, 2-a, 3-c, 4-d
- B) 1-a, 2-b, 3-c, 4-d
- C) 1-b, 2-c, 3-a, 4-d
- D) 1-c, 2-a, 3-b, 4-d
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

The chapter states: "Bab-al-Mandab Strait… connects the Red Sea to the Gulf of Aden. Also known as Gateway of Grief / Gate of Tears." "Bosphorus Strait… connects the Black Sea to the Sea of Marmara." "Palk Strait… connects the Bay of Bengal to Gulf of Mannar and separates India and Sri Lanka." "Bering Strait connects Arctic Ocean to Pacific Ocean and separates Asia and North America."

**Trap:** B reverses Bab-al-Mandab and Bosphorus. C reverses Bosphorus and Palk Strait.

**See also:** [[../../geography/19 - World Map#Straits]]
</details>

@qgeo.world_map.q6 difficulty=apply subject=geography chapter=19
The Dead Sea is bordered mainly by which two countries?

- A) Israel and Jordan
- B) Israel and Egypt
- C) Egypt and Sudan
- D) Jordan and Saudi Arabia
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

The chapter states: "Dead Sea — It is a salt water lake with highest salinity in the world (salinity: 34.2 percent). It is bordered by two main countries: Jordan and Israel."

**Trap:** B, C, D add Egypt or Saudi Arabia — none of which border the Dead Sea directly. The West Bank also borders it, but the chapter specifies Jordan and Israel.

**See also:** [[../../geography/19 - World Map#Dead Sea]]
</details>

@qgeo.world_map.q7 difficulty=apply subject=geography chapter=19
Match each city with the river it is located on:

- City 1: Paris
- City 2: London
- City 3: Cairo
- City 4: Moscow

Rivers: (a) Nile, (b) Moskva, (c) Thames, (d) Seine.

Which option correctly pairs all four?

- A) 1-d, 2-c, 3-a, 4-b
- B) 1-d, 2-a, 3-c, 4-b
- C) 1-a, 2-d, 3-c, 4-b
- D) 1-c, 2-d, 3-a, 4-b
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

The chapter table "Cities Located on Banks of River" lists: Seine river - Paris; Thames river - London; Tigris river - Baghdad; Danube river - Budapest; Nile river - Cairo; Ravi river - Lahore; Moskva river - Moscow; St. Lawrence river - Montreal/Quebec.

**Trap:** C places Cairo on the Seine (wrong). B places Cairo on the Thames (wrong). The four rivers of Europe (Seine/Thames/Moskva) and Africa (Nile) are commonly confused in world geography MCQs.

**See also:** [[../../geography/19 - World Map#CITIES LOCATED ON BANKS OF RIVER]]
</details>

The Suez Canal connects:

- A) Mediterranean Sea to the Red Sea
- B) Atlantic Ocean to the Pacific Ocean (separating North and South America)
- C) Persian Gulf to the Gulf of Oman
- D) Black Sea to the Sea of Marmara
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

The chapter states: "Suez Canal — It connects the Mediterranean Sea to the Red Sea." This is a man-made canal in Egypt.

**Trap:** B is the Panama Canal. C is the Strait of Hormuz (natural). D is the Bosphorus (natural). Candidates confuse the three artificial/natural waterways in world geography.

@qgeo.world_map.q9 difficulty=apply subject=geography chapter=19
The Indonesian capital has been moved. The NEW capital of Indonesia is:

- A) Jakarta
- B) Nusantara
- C) Kuala Lumpur
- D) Manila
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

The chapter states: "Indonesia… Capital: Nusantara (old capital: Jakarta)." Nusantara is on the island of Borneo (Kalimantan).

**Trap:** A (Jakarta) is the old capital. C is Malaysia. D is Philippines. Indonesia, Malaysia, and the Philippines are all Southeast Asian countries and their capitals are commonly confused.

@qgeo.world_map.q10 difficulty=tricky subject=geography chapter=19
Consider the following statements about the Caspian Sea:
(1) The Caspian Sea is the worlds largest lake and is a landlocked water body of Europe and Asia.
(2) The Caspian Sea is bordered by five countries: TRACK — Turkmenistan, Russia, Azerbaijan, Iran, Kazakhstan.

Which of the following is correct?

- A) Both statements are true
- B) Only statement 1 is true; statement 2 is false (Caspian is bordered by 6 countries including Iraq)
- C) Only statement 2 is true; statement 1 is false (Caspian is not a lake)
- D) Both statements are false
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

The chapter states: "Caspian Sea is a lake and is a landlocked water body of Europe and Asia… bordered by five countries (TRICK: TRACK — Turkmenistan, Russia, Azerbaijan, Iran, Kazakhstan)." Both statements are true. The chapter explicitly corrects a common confusion: Iraq does NOT border the Caspian Sea.

**Trap:** B is the classic distractor — Iraq is sometimes mistakenly listed as a Caspian country because of the "I" in the mnemonic. The chapter explicitly corrects "Iraq -> Iran".

**See also:** [[../../geography/19 - World Map#Caspian Sea]]
</details>

@qgeo.world_map.q11 difficulty=tricky subject=geography chapter=19
Consider the following about South American rivers and mountains:
(1) Mt. Aconcagua (6960 m) is the highest peak of the Andes, located in Argentina.
(2) Mt. Ojos del Salado, a dormant volcano on the Argentina-Chile border, is the highest peak in the Andes range.

Which of the following is correct?

- A) Both statements are true, but Aconcagua is the highest peak (overall)
- B) Only statement 1 is true; statement 2 is false (Ojos del Salado is on the Chile-Bolivia border)
- C) Only statement 2 is true; statement 1 is false (Aconcagua is in Chile)
- D) Both statements are false
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

Both Aconcagua (6960 m, in Argentina) and Ojos del Salado (the *highest mountain* in the Andes range, on the Argentina-Chile border) are described in the chapter. The chapter says "Ojos del Salado (corrected: Ojas -> Ojos)… is the highest mountain (dormant complex volcano) in the Andes range on Argentina-Chile border" and "Mt. Aconcagua… is the highest peak (6960 m) of Andes. It is located in Argentina."

The chapter presents Ojos del Salado as the "highest mountain" of the Andes range (because it is a complex volcano with multiple peaks) and Aconcagua as the "highest peak" (single-peak summit). For SSC purposes, Aconcagua is conventionally the answer to "highest peak of the Andes."

**Trap:** B and C each pick one statement true and the other false — but both are correct per the chapter. The distinction between "highest mountain" and "highest peak" is subtle.

**See also:** [[../../geography/19 - World Map#Andes Mountain Range]]
</details>

@qgeo.world_map.q12 difficulty=tricky subject=geography chapter=19
Assertion (A): The Ural Mountain Ranges separate Europe and Asia and are an old fold mountain range.
Reason (R): The Appalachians are among the oldest mountain ranges on Earth and are also old fold mountains.
Which of the following is correct?

- A) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A
- B) Both A and R are true, but R is NOT the correct explanation of A
- C) A is true but R is false
- D) A is false but R is false
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

Both A and R are factually correct per the chapter: "Ural Mountain Ranges — It separates Europe and Asia and is an old fold mountain range." "Appalachian Mountains — They are among the oldest mountain ranges on Earth. They are old fold mountains." R does NOT explain A — R provides a fact about the *Appalachians*, not the Urals. The Urals being old fold mountains is unrelated to the Appalachians being old fold mountains.

**Trap:** A is the obvious "looks-right" pick because both A and R are true. The test is whether R explains A — they are independent facts about two different ranges.

**See also:** [[../../geography/19 - World Map#Ural Mountain Ranges]]
</details>
