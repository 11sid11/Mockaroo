# Important Inventions and Discoveries — Questions

> Source: [[../../static-gk/19 - Important Inventions and Discoveries|Important Inventions and Discoveries]]

14 SSC CGL Tier-I MCQs on inventors, discoveries, dates and classic attribution traps.

@qgk.inventions_discoveries.q1 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=19
Who discovered X-rays?
- A) Henri Becquerel
- B) Wilhelm Conrad Röntgen
- C) J. J. Thomson
- D) Ernest Rutherford
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — Wilhelm Conrad Röntgen discovered X-rays in 1895.

**Trap:** Becquerel discovered radioactivity; Thomson discovered the electron.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/19 - Important Inventions and Discoveries#Physics]]
</details>

@@

@qgk.inventions_discoveries.q10 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=19
Which statement correctly distinguishes zero from the decimal system in the source?
- A) Brahmagupta defined rules for zero; Aryabhata is associated with the positional decimal system.
- B) Aryabhata invented the printing press; Brahmagupta discovered radioactivity.
- C) Brahmagupta developed the telephone; Aryabhata discovered X-rays.
- D) Both are credited only to Newton.
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — The source attributes the concept of zero to Aryabhata (5th century) and credits Brahmagupta with defining zero and its rules in 628 AD; it separately associates Aryabhata with the positional decimal system.

**Trap:** “Inventor of zero” is often shortened to one name, but the source distinguishes the early concept from Brahmagupta's formal rules.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/19 - Important Inventions and Discoveries#Mathematics]]
</details>

@qgk.inventions_discoveries.q11 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=19
Which sequence correctly matches particle, discoverer and year?
- A) Electron — J. J. Thomson — 1897
- B) Neutron — J. J. Thomson — 1897
- C) Proton — James Chadwick — 1932
- D) Electron — Ernest Rutherford — 1919
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Trap:** The names of subatomic-particle discoverers are commonly interchanged.

@qgk.inventions_discoveries.q12 difficulty=tricky subject=static-gk chapter=19
Who is correctly associated with both the discovery and the explanatory milestone of the photoelectric effect?
- A) Heinrich Hertz observed it; Albert Einstein explained it.
- B) Albert Einstein observed it; James Chadwick explained it.
- C) Michael Faraday observed it; Röntgen explained it.
- D) Henri Becquerel observed it; Newton explained it.
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Hertz observed the photoelectric effect in 1887, and Einstein explained it in 1905.

**Trap:** Einstein's explanation, not the initial observation, is the fact most often remembered.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/19 - Important Inventions and Discoveries#Physics]]
</details>

@qgk.inventions_discoveries.q14 difficulty=tricky subject=static-gk chapter=19
Which chronology is correct?
- A) X-rays (1895) → radioactivity (1896) → electron (1897)
- B) Electron (1897) → X-rays (1895) → radioactivity (1896)
- C) Radioactivity (1896) → X-rays (1895) → electron (1897)
- D) X-rays (1895) → electron (1897) → radioactivity (1896)
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Röntgen's X-rays (1895) preceded Becquerel's radioactivity (1896), followed by Thomson's electron (1897).

**Trap:** The three adjacent years make a simple sequence reversal easy.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/19 - Important Inventions and Discoveries#Physics]]
</details>
