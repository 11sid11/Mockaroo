# Important Temples and Pilgrimage Sites — Questions

14 SSC CGL Tier-I MCQs on Jyotirlingas, Dhams, Shakti Peethas and major pilgrimage sites.

@qgk.temples_pilgrimage.q1 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=21
Somnath, one of the 12 Jyotirlingas, is located in:
- A) Gujarat
- B) Maharashtra
- C) Rajasthan
- D) Madhya Pradesh
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Somnath is at Veraval in Gujarat.

**Trap:** Maharashtra contains several other Jyotirlingas, including Bhimashankar, Tryambakeshwar and Grishneshwar.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/21 - Important Temples and Pilgrimage Sites#12 Jyotirlingas (Lord Shiva)]]
</details>

@qgk.temples_pilgrimage.q2 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=21
Which Jyotirlinga is located at Varanasi on the Ganges?
- A) Kashi Vishwanath
- B) Kedarnath
- C) Omkareshwar
- D) Vaidyanath
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Kashi Vishwanath is the Shiva Jyotirlinga at Varanasi.

**Trap:** Kedarnath is in Uttarakhand; Omkareshwar is in Madhya Pradesh.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/21 - Important Temples and Pilgrimage Sites#12 Jyotirlingas (Lord Shiva)]]
</details>

@qgk.temples_pilgrimage.q3 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=21
Which of the following is a Char Dham (Vishnu pilgrimage) site?
- A) Puri
- B) Ujjain
- C) Nashik
- D) Deoghar
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Puri is one of the four Vishnu Char Dham sites, along with Dwarka, Badrinath and Rameshwaram.

**Trap:** Ujjain, Nashik and Deoghar are associated with Jyotirlingas.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/21 - Important Temples and Pilgrimage Sites#4 Dhams (Char Dham — Vishnu pilgrimage)]]
</details>

@qgk.temples_pilgrimage.q4 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=21
The Golden Temple is located in:
- A) Amritsar, Punjab
- B) Patna, Bihar
- C) Nanded, Maharashtra
- D) Delhi
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — The Golden Temple (Harmandir Sahib) is in Amritsar, Punjab.

**Trap:** Patna Sahib is a major Sikh site in Bihar; Nanded is associated with Hazur Sahib.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/21 - Important Temples and Pilgrimage Sites#Sikh Gurdwaras (Holy Sites)]]
</details>

@qgk.temples_pilgrimage.q5 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=21
A pilgrim wants to visit the only south-facing Jyotirlinga, at Ujjain. Which temple is it?
- A) Mahakaleshwar
- B) Omkareshwar
- C) Somnath
- D) Nageshwar
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Mahakaleshwar at Ujjain is the only south-facing (Dakshinamukhi) Jyotirlinga.

**Trap:** Omkareshwar is also in Madhya Pradesh but lies on an island shaped like “Om”.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/21 - Important Temples and Pilgrimage Sites#12 Jyotirlingas (Lord Shiva)]]
</details>

@qgk.temples_pilgrimage.q6 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=21
Which site is correctly paired with the river whose source is associated with it?
- A) Tryambakeshwar — Godavari
- B) Somnath — Yamuna
- C) Kashi Vishwanath — Narmada
- D) Bhimashankar — Ganga
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Tryambakeshwar near Nashik is associated with the source of the Godavari.

**Trap:** Bhimashankar is associated with the Bhima River; Kashi Vishwanath is on the Ganges.

@qgk.temples_pilgrimage.q7 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=21
Kamakhya Temple, one of the best-known Shakti Peethas, is in:
- A) Assam
- B) West Bengal
- C) Odisha
- D) Himachal Pradesh
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Kamakhya Temple is in Guwahati, Assam.

**Trap:** Kalighat is in Kolkata, West Bengal; Jwalaji is in Himachal Pradesh.

@qgk.temples_pilgrimage.q8 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=21
Which is the correct distinction between the two Char Dham lists in the source?
- A) Vishnu Char Dham includes Puri, Dwarka, Badrinath and Rameshwaram; Himalayan Char Dham includes Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath.
- B) Both lists contain exactly the same four temples.
- C) Vishnu Char Dham excludes Badrinath and includes Somnath.
- D) Himalayan Char Dham includes Puri and Dwarka instead of the Himalayan sites.
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — The source distinguishes the pan-Indian Vishnu list from the Himalayan pilgrimage circuit; Badrinath appears in both.

**Trap:** The shared Badrinath entry can make the two lists look identical.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/21 - Important Temples and Pilgrimage Sites#4 Dhams (Char Dham — Vishnu pilgrimage)]]
</details>

@qgk.temples_pilgrimage.q9 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=21
The temple called Baba Dham at Deoghar is which Jyotirlinga?
- A) Vaidyanath
- B) Grishneshwar
- C) Mallikarjuna
- D) Nageshwar
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Vaidyanath (Baidyanath) Jyotirlinga is at Deoghar in Jharkhand and is also called Baba Dham.

**Trap:** Nageshwar is at Dwarka, while Grishneshwar is near Ellora.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/21 - Important Temples and Pilgrimage Sites#12 Jyotirlingas (Lord Shiva)]]
