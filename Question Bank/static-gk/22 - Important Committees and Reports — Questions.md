12 SSC CGL Tier-I MCQs on commissions, chairs, years and purposes.

@qgk.committees_reports.q1 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=22
The Drafting Committee of the Constituent Assembly was chaired by:
- A) Jawaharlal Nehru
- B) B. R. Ambedkar
- C) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
- D) Rajendra Prasad
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — B. R. Ambedkar chaired the Drafting Committee.

**Trap:** Nehru chaired the Union Powers and Union Constitution Committees; Patel chaired the Provincial Constitution Committee.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/22 - Important Committees and Reports#Constitution-Making Committees (Constituent Assembly)]]
</details>

@qgk.committees_reports.q2 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=22
The Hunter Commission of 1882 dealt primarily with:
- A) Education
- B) Labour conditions
- C) Centre–State relations
- D) Police reforms
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — The 1882 Hunter Commission was the Indian Education Commission.

**Trap:** The 1919 Hunter Committee investigated the Jallianwala Bagh massacre; the two are not the same inquiry.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/22 - Important Committees and Reports#Pre-Independence]]
</details>

@qgk.committees_reports.q3 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=22
