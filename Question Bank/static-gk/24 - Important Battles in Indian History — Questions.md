# Important Battles in Indian History — Questions

> Source: [[../../static-gk/24 - Important Battles in Indian History|Important Battles in Indian History]]

12 SSC CGL Tier-I MCQs on battle years, combatants, victors and historical consequences.

@qgk.battles.q1 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=24
The Battle of Kalinga was fought in:
- A) 326 BCE
- B) 261 BCE
- C) 1192 CE
- D) 1526 CE
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — Ashoka's Battle of Kalinga took place in 261 BCE.

**Trap:** 326 BCE is associated with Alexander's campaign; 1526 is the First Battle of Panipat.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/24 - Important Battles in Indian History#Ancient Indian Battles]]
</details>

@qgk.battles.q2 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=24
Who won the First Battle of Panipat (1526)?
- A) Ibrahim Lodi
- B) Babur
- C) Akbar
- D) Sher Shah Suri
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — Babur defeated Ibrahim Lodi in 1526 and founded the Mughal Empire.

**Trap:** Akbar won the Second Battle of Panipat in 1556.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/24 - Important Battles in Indian History#Medieval Indian Battles]]
</details>

@qgk.battles.q3 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=24
The Battle of Plassey was fought in:

@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — The Battle of Plassey took place in 1757.

**Trap:** The Battle of Buxar followed in 1764; 1761 was the Third Battle of Panipat.

@qgk.battles.q4 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=24
The Battle of Saraighat (1671) was won by:
- A) The Mughals under Mir Jumla
- B) The Ahoms under Lachit Borphukan
- C) The Marathas under Shivaji
- D) The British under Munro
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — The Ahom Kingdom, led by Lachit Borphukan, defeated the Mughals at Saraighat.

**Trap:** Mir Jumla represents the Mughal side, which lost the battle.

@qgk.battles.q5 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=24
A historian wants the battle that led Ashoka to turn toward Buddhism after a devastating victory. Which battle is intended?
- A) Kalinga
- B) Haldighati
- C) Khanwa
- D) Saraighat
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Ashoka's victory at Kalinga in 261 BCE caused remorse and is linked to his adoption of Buddhism.

**Trap:** Haldighati concerns Rana Pratap and the Mughals; it is not the Ashokan turning point.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/24 - Important Battles in Indian History#Ancient Indian Battles]]
</details>

@qgk.battles.q6 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=24
Which battle did Muhammad Ghori win in 1192 in the decisive contest against Prithviraj Chauhan?
- A) First Battle of Tarain
- B) Second Battle of Tarain
- C) Battle of Chandawar
- D) Battle of Khanwa
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — Muhammad Ghori won the Second Battle of Tarain in 1192; the first battle in 1191 was won by Prithviraj Chauhan's Rajput forces.

**Trap:** Chandawar (1194) was another Ghori victory, against Jaichand, but it was not the decisive contest against Prithviraj.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/24 - Important Battles in Indian History#Medieval Indian Battles]]
</details>

@qgk.battles.q10 difficulty=tricky subject=static-gk chapter=24
Which sequence correctly matches the three Battles of Panipat?
- A) 1526—Babur vs Ibrahim Lodi; 1556—Akbar's forces vs Hemu; 1761—Abdali vs Marathas
- B) 1526—Akbar vs Hemu; 1556—Babur vs Lodi; 1761—British vs Marathas
- C) 1526—Babur vs Rana Sanga; 1556—Ghori vs Prithviraj; 1761—Clive vs Siraj
- D) 1526—Lodi won; 1556—Hemu won; 1761—Marathas won
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Those are the correct years and combatant pairings for Panipat I, II and III.

**Trap:** Babur versus Rana Sanga was Khanwa, while Ghori versus Prithviraj was Tarain.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/24 - Important Battles in Indian History#Key concepts]]
</details>

@qgk.battles.q11 difficulty=tricky subject=static-gk chapter=24
Which pair is incorrectly matched?
- A) Plassey — 1757 — Robert Clive
- B) Buxar — 1764 — British victory
- C) Third Panipat — 1761 — Ahmad Shah Abdali
- D) Second Tarain — 1191 — Muhammad Ghori's victory
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D** — Ghori won the Second Battle of Tarain in 1192; Prithviraj's side won the first battle in 1191.

**Trap:** The two Tarain years and victors are a standard SSC reversal.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/24 - Important Battles in Indian History#Key concepts]]
</details>

@qgk.battles.q12 difficulty=tricky subject=static-gk chapter=24
Which chronology is correct from earliest to latest?
- A) Kalinga → Second Tarain → First Panipat → Plassey → Buxar
- B) Second Tarain → Kalinga → Plassey → First Panipat → Buxar
- C) Kalinga → First Panipat → Second Tarain → Buxar → Plassey
- D) First Panipat → Kalinga → Second Tarain → Plassey → Buxar
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — The years are 261 BCE, 1192, 1526, 1757 and 1764 respectively.

**Trap:** Plassey precedes Buxar by seven years; Tarain precedes Panipat by more than three centuries.
