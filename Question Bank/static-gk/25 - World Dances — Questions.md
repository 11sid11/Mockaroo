# World Dances — Questions

> Source: [[../../static-gk/25 - World Dances|World Dances]]

SSC CGL Tier-I MCQs on world dance–country associations and commonly confused origins.

@qgk.world_dances.q1 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=25
Tango is principally associated with which country?
- A) Argentina
- B) Brazil
- C) Cuba
- D) Spain
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Tango originated in the Río de la Plata region and is chiefly associated with Argentina.

**Trap:** Brazil is associated with Samba; Spain with Flamenco.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/25 - World Dances#Dance vs. Country (frequently asked)]]
</details>

@qgk.world_dances.q2 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=25
The Waltz is traditionally associated with:
- A) Vienna, Austria
- B) Warsaw, Poland
- C) Prague, Czech Republic
- D) Paris, France
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — The Waltz developed as a ballroom dance in Vienna, Austria.

**Trap:** Mazurka is Polish and Polka is associated with Bohemia.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/25 - World Dances#Dance vs. Country (frequently asked)]]
</details>

@qgk.world_dances.q3 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=25
Which dance originated in Bohemia and is associated with the Czech Republic?
- A) Polka
- B) Mazurka
- C) Fandango
- D) Merengue
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Polka is a partnered folk dance from Bohemia, now part of the Czech Republic.

**Trap:** Mazurka is Polish; Merengue is Dominican.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/25 - World Dances#Dance vs. Country (frequently asked)]]
</details>

@qgk.world_dances.q4 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=25
Hula is a storytelling dance tradition of:
- A) Hawaii, USA
- B) New Zealand
- C) Japan
- D) Ireland
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Hula is a Polynesian storytelling dance associated with Hawaii.

**Trap:** Haka is the Māori performance tradition associated with New Zealand.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/25 - World Dances#Dance vs. Country (frequently asked)]]
</details>

@qgk.world_dances.q5 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=25
A dance combines *cante* (singing), guitar, dance and *palmas* (handclaps). Which dance is it?
- A) Flamenco
- B) Samba
- C) Rumba
- D) Waltz
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Flamenco is the Andalusian Spanish tradition combining singing, guitar, dance and handclaps.

**Trap:** Samba is Brazilian and is associated with Rio Carnival.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/25 - World Dances#Dance vs. Country (frequently asked)]]

@qgk.world_dances.q11 difficulty=tricky subject=static-gk chapter=25
Which statements are correctly matched?
1. Paso Doble — Spain
2. Jota — Spain
3. Sardana — Catalonia, Spain
- A) 1 only
- B) 1 and 2 only
- C) 2 and 3 only
- D) 1, 2 and 3
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D** — All three are Spanish traditions: Jota is associated with Aragon and Sardana with Catalonia.

**Trap:** Spain has several distinct regional dances, not one single national style in the list.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/25 - World Dances#Dance vs. Country (frequently asked)]]
</details>

@qgk.world_dances.q12 difficulty=tricky subject=static-gk chapter=25
Which statement about Ballet is most accurate according to the chapter?
- A) It originated in Russia and never developed elsewhere.
- B) It originated in the Italian Renaissance and later developed prominently in France and Russia.
- C) It originated in Cuba as a Latin ballroom dance.
- D) It originated in Austria as another name for Waltz.
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — Ballet began in Renaissance Italy and later developed prominently in France and Russia.

**Trap:** Russia is strongly associated with ballet's development, but not its origin.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/25 - World Dances#Top 20 most-asked in SSC]]
</details>
