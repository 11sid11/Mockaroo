@qgk.revision.q1 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=26
Which is India's official National Game?
- A) Hockey
- B) Cricket
- C) Kabaddi
- D) There is no official National Game
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D** - The revision chapter states that India has no official national game.

**Trap:** Hockey is the familiar but incorrect claim.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/14 - National Symbols of India#Trivia (often asked in SSC)]]
</details>

@qgk.revision.q2 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=26
Who was India's first President?
- A) S. Radhakrishnan
- B) Rajendra Prasad
- C) Jawaharlal Nehru
- D) Vallabhbhai Patel
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** - Dr. Rajendra Prasad was India's first President.

**Trap:** Radhakrishnan was the first Vice-President, while Nehru was the first Prime Minister.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/15 - First in India and World#Political and Constitutional]]
</details>

@qgk.revision.q3 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=26
Which line separates India and China in the eastern sector?
- A) Radcliffe Line
- B) Durand Line
- C) McMahon Line
- D) Line of Control
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C** - The McMahon Line is associated with the eastern India-China boundary.

**Trap:** Radcliffe and LoC concern India-Pakistan; Durand concerns Afghanistan-Pakistan.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/17 - Boundary Lines of India#Historical Boundary Lines]]
</details>

@qgk.revision.q5 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=26

- C) K. D. Jadhav
- D) Rajyavardhan Singh Rathore
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** - Abhinav Bindra won India's first individual Olympic gold in 2008.

**Trap:** Neeraj Chopra was the first Indian athletics gold medallist; K. D. Jadhav won a post-independence bronze.

@qgk.revision.q13 difficulty=tricky subject=static-gk chapter=26

- B) Samba - Brazil
- C) Flamenco - Spain
- D) Waltz - Poland
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D** - The Waltz is associated with Austria, especially Vienna; Mazurka is associated with Poland.

**Trap:** Waltz and Mazurka are both partnered dances but have different country associations.
