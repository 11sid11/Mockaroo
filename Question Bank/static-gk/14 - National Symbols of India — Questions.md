# National Symbols of India — Questions

> Source: [[../../static-gk/14 - National Symbols of India|National Symbols of India]]

SSC CGL Tier-I MCQs on India's official symbols, adoption dates, authors, and status traps.

@qgk.national_symbols.q1 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=14
Who designed India's National Flag, the Tiranga?
- A) Nandalal Bose
- B) Pingali Venkayya
- C) B. R. Ambedkar
- D) Rabindranath Tagore
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — Pingali Venkayya designed the Tiranga; it was adopted on 22 July 1947.

**Trap:** The distractors are distinguished by the source's India-specific firsts; Nandalal Bose is not needed for this question.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/14 - National Symbols of India#Comprehensive list of National Symbols]]
</details>

@qgk.national_symbols.q2 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=14
“Satyameva Jayate”, India's national motto, is taken from which text?
- A) Isha Upanishad
- B) Mundaka Upanishad
- C) Bhagavad Gita
- D) Rigveda
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — The motto means “Truth Alone Triumphs” and comes from the Mundaka Upanishad.

**Trap:** Its Sanskrit wording does not make it a quotation from the Bhagavad Gita.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/14 - National Symbols of India#Comprehensive list of National Symbols]]
</details>

@qgk.national_symbols.q3 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=14
What is the first month of India's Saka National Calendar?
- A) Vaisakha
- B) Chaitra
- C) Kartika
- D) Phalguna
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — Chaitra is the first month; Chaitra 1 normally corresponds to 22 March.

**Trap:** Vaisakha follows Chaitra and is not the opening month.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/14 - National Symbols of India#Comprehensive list of National Symbols]]
</details>

@qgk.national_symbols.q4 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=14
Which is India's National Heritage Animal?
- A) One-horned rhinoceros
- B) Asiatic lion
- C) Indian elephant
- D) Royal Bengal tiger
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C** — The Indian elephant was adopted as the National Heritage Animal in 2010.

**Trap:** The Royal Bengal tiger is the National Animal, a different designation.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/14 - National Symbols of India#Comprehensive list of National Symbols]]
</details>

@qgk.national_symbols.q5 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=14
A quiz describes a national symbol as *Pavo cristatus*, adopted in 1963. Which is it?
- A) Indian peacock
- B) King cobra
- C) Ganges river dolphin
- D) Royal Bengal tiger
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — *Pavo cristatus* is the Indian peacock, India's National Bird.

**Trap:** *Panthera tigris* is the tiger and *Ophiophagus hannah* is the king cobra.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/14 - National Symbols of India#Comprehensive list of National Symbols]]
</details>

@qgk.national_symbols.q6 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=14
Which event and symbol are correctly associated with 1973?
- A) Project Tiger — Royal Bengal tiger
- B) Project Elephant — Indian elephant
- C) Calendar Reform — Saka Calendar
- D) Ganga declaration — National River
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — The tiger became the National Animal in 1973, the year Project Tiger began.

**Trap:** The Saka Calendar dates to 1957, the elephant to 2010, and the Ganga to 2008.

@qgk.national_symbols.q7 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=14
A programme lasts about 52 seconds and uses the composition first sung at the Calcutta INC session in 1911. What is performed?
- A) Vande Mataram
- B) Sare Jahan Se Achha
- C) Jana Gana Mana
- D) The National Pledge
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C** — Jana Gana Mana, written by Rabindranath Tagore, was first sung on 27 December 1911 and takes about 52 seconds.

**Trap:** Vande Mataram is the National Song and comes from *Anandamath*.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/14 - National Symbols of India#Jana Gana Mana — quick facts]]
</details>

@qgk.national_symbols.q8 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=14
Which author–work–symbol combination is correct?
- A) Rabindranath Tagore — *Anandamath* — National Song
- B) Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay — *Anandamath* — National Song
- C) Pingali Venkayya — *Bharat Bhagya Vidhata* — National Anthem
- D) Pydimarri Venkata Subba Rao — *Anandamath* — National Pledge
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay wrote Vande Mataram in *Anandamath* (1882), India's National Song.

**Trap:** Tagore wrote Jana Gana Mana; Pydimarri Venkata Subba Rao wrote the National Pledge.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/14 - National Symbols of India#Vande Mataram — quick facts]]
</details>

@qgk.national_symbols.q9 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=14
Which statement about India's State Emblem is correct?
- A) It was adopted on 15 August 1947 from the Sanchi Lion Capital.
- B) It shows all four lions at once and omits the motto.
- C) It was adopted on 26 January 1950 from the Lion Capital at Sarnath.
- D) Its motto is taken from the Bhagavad Gita.
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C** — The emblem was adopted on 26 January 1950 and adapted from Ashoka's Lion Capital at Sarnath.

**Trap:** Four lions belong to the original capital, but three are visible in the emblem; the motto is from the Mundaka Upanishad.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/14 - National Symbols of India#State Emblem – what is shown and what's NOT]]
</details>

@qgk.national_symbols.q10 difficulty=tricky subject=static-gk chapter=14
What is India's official national game?
- A) Hockey
- B) Cricket
- C) Kabaddi
- D) India has no official national game
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D** — India has no officially designated national game; a 2012 government RTI reply confirmed this.

**Trap:** Hockey is the familiar but unofficial answer.

@qgk.national_symbols.q12 difficulty=tricky subject=static-gk chapter=14

- A) A 2023 amendment replaced “India” with “Bharat”.
- B) Only “India” is constitutionally valid.
- C) Article 1 says “India, that is Bharat”; both names remain valid.
- D) “Bharat” became valid only after the G20 invitation used it.
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C** — Article 1 already says “India, that is Bharat”; the G20 wording did not formally rename the country.
