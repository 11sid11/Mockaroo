12 SSC CGL Tier-I MCQs on famous Indian and world nicknames, with confusable epithets.

@qgk.nicknames.q1 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=20
Who is known as “Netaji”?
- A) Subhas Chandra Bose
- B) Jawaharlal Nehru
- C) Vallabhbhai Patel
- D) Jayaprakash Narayan
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Subhas Chandra Bose is popularly known as Netaji.

**Trap:** “Lok Nayak” belongs to Jayaprakash Narayan.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/20 - Nicknames of Famous Personalities#Indian Freedom Fighters and Political Leaders]]
</details>

@qgk.nicknames.q2 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=20

@qgk.nicknames.q11 difficulty=tricky subject=static-gk chapter=20
Which option correctly distinguishes the “Father of the Nation” labels listed in the source?
- A) Mahatma Gandhi — India; George Washington — USA
- B) George Washington — India; Mahatma Gandhi — USA
- C) Both titles refer only to Jinnah
- D) Neither personality has that association
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — The source lists Mahatma Gandhi as Father of the Nation of India and George Washington as Father of the Nation of the USA.

**Trap:** “Father of the Nation” is not a universal nickname for one person worldwide.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/20 - Nicknames of Famous Personalities#Quick recall — top 25 most-asked]]

@qgk.nicknames.q12 difficulty=tricky subject=static-gk chapter=20
Which set contains only correctly matched nicknames?
- A) Tilak—Lokmanya; C. R. Das—Deshbandhu; Jinnah—Quaid-e-Azam
- B) Tilak—Netaji; C. R. Das—Punjab Kesari; Jinnah—Lok Nayak
- C) Tilak—Flying Sikh; C. R. Das—Iron Man; Jinnah—Bard of Avon
- D) Tilak—Missile Man; C. R. Das—Mahamana; Jinnah—Master Blaster
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Bal Gangadhar Tilak was Lokmanya, Chittaranjan Das Deshbandhu, and Muhammad Ali Jinnah Quaid-e-Azam.

**Trap:** The other choices mix titles from unrelated freedom fighters, scientists and sportspersons.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/20 - Nicknames of Famous Personalities#Quick recall — top 25 most-asked]]
</details>
