15 SSC CGL Tier-I MCQs on political, scientific, sporting, cinematic and global firsts.

@qgk.firsts.q1 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=15
Who was the first Speaker of the Lok Sabha?
- A) G. V. Mavalankar
- B) M. Ananthasayanam Ayyangar
- C) Hukam Singh
- D) Neelam Sanjiva Reddy
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — G. V. Mavalankar served as the first Lok Sabha Speaker from 1952 to 1956.

**Trap:** Ayyangar succeeded Mavalankar and was not the first.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/15 - First in India and World#Political and Constitutional]]
</details>

@qgk.firsts.q4 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=15
India's first satellite was:

- B) INSAT-1A

- D) Bhaskara-I
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

@qgk.firsts.q10 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=15

- B) Alam Ara

- D) Pundalik

@qgk.firsts.q13 difficulty=tricky subject=static-gk chapter=15
Which sequence correctly matches the first person in space, first woman in space and first person to walk on the Moon?
- A) Yuri Gagarin (1961); Valentina Tereshkova (1963); Neil Armstrong (1969)
- B) Neil Armstrong (1961); Yuri Gagarin (1963); Valentina Tereshkova (1969)
- C) Yuri Gagarin (1963); Neil Armstrong (1969); Valentina Tereshkova (1961)
- D) Valentina Tereshkova (1961); Yuri Gagarin (1969); Neil Armstrong (1963)
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Yuri Gagarin was first in space (1961), Valentina Tereshkova first woman in space (1963), and Neil Armstrong first to walk on the Moon (1969).

**Trap:** The three names are all space pioneers, but their milestones and years are different.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/15 - First in India and World#Science and Technology]]
</details>

@qgk.firsts.q14 difficulty=tricky subject=static-gk chapter=15
Who was the first woman to win a Nobel Prize, and in which year?
- A) Mother Teresa, 1979
- B) Marie Curie, 1903
- C) Valentina Tereshkova, 1963
- D) Indira Gandhi, 1971
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — Marie Curie was the first woman Nobel laureate, winning in 1903; Mother Teresa is listed separately as the first Indian-born woman Nobel laureate (1979).

**Trap:** Mother Teresa's 1979 Nobel is a first associated with Indian-born recipients, not the world's first woman Nobel Prize.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/15 - First in India and World#Cultural]]
</details>

@qgk.firsts.q15 difficulty=tricky subject=static-gk chapter=15
Which statement correctly distinguishes the first film ever made from India's first film?
- A) *Roundhay Garden Scene* was the first film ever made; *Raja Harishchandra* was India's first film.
- B) *Raja Harishchandra* was the first film ever made; *The Jazz Singer* was India's first.
- C) *Alam Ara* was the first film ever made; *Raja Harishchandra* was India's first sound film.
- D) *The Story of the Kelly Gang* was the first film ever made; *Alam Ara* was India's first silent film.
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — The source identifies Louis Le Prince's *Roundhay Garden Scene* (1888) as the first film ever made and Dadasaheb Phalke's *Raja Harishchandra* (1913) as India's first film.

**Trap:** *Alam Ara* was India's first sound film, not its first silent film.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/15 - First in India and World#Cinema and Media]]
</details>
