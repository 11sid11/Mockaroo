# Countries - Capitals Currencies and Languages — Questions

> Source: [[../../static-gk/16 - Countries - Capitals Currencies and Languages|Countries - Capitals Currencies and Languages]]

15 SSC CGL Tier-I MCQs on high-frequency country, capital, currency and language combinations.

@qgk.countries.q1 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=16
What is the capital of Kazakhstan?
- A) Bishkek
- B) Astana
- C) Ashgabat
- D) Tashkent
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — Kazakhstan's capital is Astana (formerly Nur-Sultan).

**Trap:** Bishkek is Kyrgyzstan; Ashgabat is Turkmenistan.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/16 - Countries - Capitals Currencies and Languages#Asia]]
</details>

@qgk.countries.q2 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=16
The currency of Japan is:
- A) Won
- B) Yuan
- C) Yen
- D) Ringgit
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C** — Japan uses the Yen (JPY).

**Trap:** The Won belongs to North and South Korea; Renminbi is China's currency.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/16 - Countries - Capitals Currencies and Languages#Asia]]
</details>

@qgk.countries.q3 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=16
Which language is listed as the official language of Iran in the source table?
- A) Arabic
- B) Persian (Farsi)
- C) Turkish
- D) Pashto
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — Iran's language is Persian (Farsi), and its capital is Tehran.

**Trap:** Arabic is associated with Iraq and many Gulf states; Pashto is associated with Afghanistan.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/16 - Countries - Capitals Currencies and Languages#Asia]]
</details>

@qgk.countries.q4 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=16
Which country uses the currency called the Taka?
- A) Bangladesh
- B) Bhutan
- C) Nepal
- D) Myanmar
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Bangladesh uses the Taka (BDT) and has Dhaka as its capital.

**Trap:** Bhutan uses the Ngultrum; Nepal uses the Nepalese Rupee.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/16 - Countries - Capitals Currencies and Languages#Asia]]
</details>

@qgk.countries.q5 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=16
Which country has Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte as its legislative capital?
- A) Maldives
- B) Sri Lanka
- C) Nepal
- D) Singapore
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — Sri Lanka's legislative capital is Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte; Colombo is its commercial capital.

**Trap:** Malé is the capital of Maldives, while Kathmandu is Nepal's capital.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/16 - Countries - Capitals Currencies and Languages#Asia]]
</details>

@qgk.countries.q6 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=16
A traveller visits a country whose capital is Ashgabat and whose currency is the Manat. Which country is it?
- A) Azerbaijan
- B) Turkmenistan
- C) Kazakhstan
- D) Uzbekistan
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — Ashgabat is the capital of Turkmenistan, whose currency is the Manat (TMT).

**Trap:** Azerbaijan also uses a manat, but its capital is Baku.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/16 - Countries - Capitals Currencies and Languages#Asia]]
</details>

@qgk.countries.q7 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=16
Which country–capital–currency combination is correct?
- A) Oman — Muscat — Omani Rial
- B) Qatar — Riyadh — Qatari Riyal
- C) Saudi Arabia — Doha — Saudi Riyal
- D) Kuwait — Manama — Kuwaiti Dinar
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Trap:** Doha is Qatar, Riyadh is Saudi Arabia, Manama is Bahrain, and Kuwait City is Kuwait.

@qgk.countries.q8 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=16

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

@qgk.countries.q13 difficulty=tricky subject=static-gk chapter=16
Which statement about Malaysia is correct?
- A) Putrajaya is the national capital and Kuala Lumpur has no capital role.
- B) Kuala Lumpur is the capital, while Putrajaya is the administrative centre.
- C) Penang is the capital and Kuala Lumpur is judicial only.

@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — Kuala Lumpur is Malaysia's capital; Putrajaya is the administrative centre.

**Trap:** Questions sometimes replace the constitutional/national capital with the administrative seat.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/16 - Countries - Capitals Currencies and Languages#Asia]]
</details>

@qgk.countries.q14 difficulty=tricky subject=static-gk chapter=16
Which pair of countries both use a currency named “Manat”?
- A) Azerbaijan and Turkmenistan
- B) Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan
- C) Tajikistan and Uzbekistan
- D) Iran and Oman
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Azerbaijan and Turkmenistan both call their currencies the Manat, with different currency codes.

**Trap:** Kazakhstan uses Tenge; Kyrgyzstan and Uzbekistan use currencies called Som.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/16 - Countries - Capitals Currencies and Languages#Asia]]
</details>

- C) Thailand—Kyat; Vietnam—Kip; Cambodia—Baht
- D) Thailand—Taka; Vietnam—Rupiah; Cambodia—Yen
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Thailand uses the Baht, Vietnam the Đồng, and Cambodia the Riel.

**Trap:** The distractors rotate several neighbouring Asian currencies among the wrong countries.
