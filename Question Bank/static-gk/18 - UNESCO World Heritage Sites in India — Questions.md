@qgk.unesco_sites.q1 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=18
As stated in the source, how many UNESCO World Heritage Sites does India have as of 2025?
- A) 35
- B) 40
- C) 43
- D) 50
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C** — The source gives India 43 World Heritage Sites as of 2025: 35 cultural, 7 natural and 1 mixed.

**Trap:** 35 is only the cultural-site count.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/18 - UNESCO World Heritage Sites in India#Summary at a glance]]
</details>

@qgk.unesco_sites.q2 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=18
Which was India's first World Heritage Site addition according to the source?
- A) Taj Mahal
- B) Agra Fort
- C) Qutub Minar
- D) Ajanta Caves
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — The source lists Agra Fort as the first addition in its summary table; all four options were included in the 1983 first batch.

**Trap:** The year alone cannot distinguish the first batch members, so use the source's explicit “1st: Agra Fort” wording.

</details>

@qgk.unesco_sites.q3 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=18
Which is India's only mixed World Heritage Site?
- A) Western Ghats
- B) Khangchendzonga National Park
- C) Kaziranga National Park
- D) Sundarbans National Park
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B** — Khangchendzonga National Park is India's only mixed (cultural and natural) World Heritage Site.

**Trap:** The other three are natural sites.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/18 - UNESCO World Heritage Sites in India#Mixed Sites (1)]]
</details>

@qgk.unesco_sites.q4 difficulty=recall subject=static-gk chapter=18

@qgk.unesco_sites.q5 difficulty=apply subject=static-gk chapter=18
A visitor wants to see prehistoric cave paintings at a UNESCO site. Which site should be selected?
- A) Rock Shelters of Bhimbetka
- B) Rani ki vav
- C) Nalanda Mahavihara
- D) Dholavira
@@
<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A** — Bhimbetka in Madhya Pradesh is known for prehistoric rock-shelter paintings.

**Trap:** Rani ki vav is a Gujarat stepwell; Dholavira is a Harappan site.

**See also:** [[../../static-gk/18 - UNESCO World Heritage Sites in India#North India]]
</details>
