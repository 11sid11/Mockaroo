@qbio.bone.q1 difficulty=recall subject=biology chapter=21
The commonly accepted total numbers of bones in an adult human and a child are respectively:

- A) 206 and about 300
- B) 300 and 206
- C) 126 and 80
- D) 208 and 270
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

An adult skeleton has 206 bones, whereas a child's skeleton has about 300. The count falls because several bones fuse during growth.

**Trap:** B reverses the age order; growth causes fusion, not the creation of nearly a hundred additional separate bones.

**See also:** [[../../biology/21 - Bone#TOTAL BONE COUNT]]
</details>

@qbio.bone.q2 difficulty=recall subject=biology chapter=21
The adult axial and appendicular skeletons contain how many bones respectively?

- A) 126 and 80
- B) 80 and 126
- C) 86 and 120
- D) 78 and 128
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

The axial skeleton contains 80 bones and the appendicular skeleton 126; together they total 206.

**Trap:** A simply reverses the two correct counts.

**See also:** [[../../biology/21 - Bone#SKELETAL SYSTEM — OVERVIEW]]
</details>

@qbio.bone.q3 difficulty=recall subject=biology chapter=21
Which sequence correctly gives the numbers of cervical, thoracic, and lumbar vertebrae?

- A) 5, 12, 7
- B) 7, 5, 12
- C) 7, 12, 5
- D) 12, 7, 5
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C**

The vertebral regions contain 7 cervical, 12 thoracic, and 5 lumbar vertebrae. The first two cervical vertebrae are atlas and axis.

**Trap:** A swaps the cervical and lumbar counts, a common error because both are small numbers.

**See also:** [[../../biology/21 - Bone#Bones in Spinal Cord / Vertebral Column (26 in adults)]]
</details>

@qbio.bone.q4 difficulty=recall subject=biology chapter=21
One human hand contains:

- A) 7 carpals, 5 metacarpals, and 14 phalanges
- B) 8 carpals, 5 metacarpals, and 14 phalanges
- C) 8 tarsals, 5 metatarsals, and 14 phalanges
- D) 5 carpals, 8 metacarpals, and 14 phalanges
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

A hand has 8 carpals, 5 metacarpals, and 14 phalanges, making 27 bones in all.

**Trap:** A borrows the count of seven tarsals from the foot and assigns it to the wrist.

**See also:** [[../../biology/21 - Bone#Hand (27 per side; 54 total)]]
</details>

@qbio.bone.q5 difficulty=apply subject=biology chapter=21
A fracture affects the central chest bone to which ribs connect through cartilage. Which bone is injured?

- A) Scapula
- B) Sternum
- C) Clavicle
- D) Hyoid
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

The sternum is the central chest bone associated with the 12 pairs of ribs through cartilage.

**Trap:** The scapula and clavicle are shoulder-girdle bones, not the central anchor of the rib cage.

**See also:** [[../../biology/21 - Bone#Bones in Rib Cage (24 + 1 = 25)]]
</details>

@qbio.bone.q6 difficulty=apply subject=biology chapter=21
A patient breaks both bones of the forearm. Which pair has most likely been fractured?

- A) Humerus and radius
- B) Radius and ulna
- C) Tibia and fibula
- D) Femur and patella
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

The radius and ulna are the paired forearm bones. The humerus is in the upper arm.

**Trap:** A includes one forearm bone but pairs it with the humerus, which lies above the elbow.

**See also:** [[../../biology/21 - Bone#Arm (3 per side; 6 total)]]
</details>

@qbio.bone.q7 difficulty=apply subject=biology chapter=21
A bone belongs to the framework chiefly protecting the brain and spinal cord rather than providing limb leverage. It belongs to the:

- A) Appendicular skeleton
- B) Axial skeleton
- C) Shoulder girdle only
- D) Pelvic girdle only
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

The axial skeleton includes the skull and vertebral column and protects the brain and spinal cord. The appendicular skeleton primarily supports limb movement.

**Trap:** A is associated with locomotion and leverage, wording deliberately contrasted with protection in the stem.

**See also:** [[../../biology/21 - Bone#AXIAL SKELETON (80 bones)]]
</details>

@qbio.bone.q8 difficulty=apply subject=biology chapter=21
A complete adult rib cage is counted as 12 pairs of ribs plus one sternum. How many bones does this make under the chapter's scheme?

- A) 12
- B) 24
- C) 25
- D) 26
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C**

Twelve pairs give 24 ribs; adding one sternum gives 25 bones.

**Trap:** B counts the ribs correctly but omits the sternum explicitly included in the question.

**See also:** [[../../biology/21 - Bone#Bones in Rib Cage (24 + 1 = 25)]]

@qbio.bone.q9 difficulty=apply subject=biology chapter=21
If both hands are excluded from the appendicular skeleton, how many appendicular bones remain?

- A) 54
- B) 72
- C) 80
- D) 152
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

The appendicular skeleton has 126 bones. Both hands contain 54, so 126 − 54 = 72 remain.

**Trap:** A gives only the number removed; it does not answer how many remain.

**See also:** [[../../biology/21 - Bone#APPENDICULAR SKELETON (126 bones)]]
</details>

@qbio.bone.q10 difficulty=tricky subject=biology chapter=21
Consider the following pairs:
1. Atlas — C1
2. Axis — C2
3. Patella — kneecap
4. Hyoid — supports the tongue

How many pairs are correctly matched?

- A) One
- B) Two
- C) Three
- D) Four
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

All four pairs are correct: atlas and axis are C1 and C2, the patella is the kneecap, and the hyoid supports the tongue.

**Trap:** Atlas and axis are frequently reversed, which can make an otherwise correct set seem to contain only three matches.

**See also:** [[../../biology/21 - Bone#Bones in Spinal Cord / Vertebral Column (26 in adults)]] · [[../../biology/21 - Bone#Hyoid Bone]]
</details>

@qbio.bone.q11 difficulty=tricky subject=biology chapter=21
Which calculation correctly accounts for all 80 bones of the axial skeleton?

- A) Skull 22 + ear 6 + spine 26 + rib cage 25 + hyoid 1
- B) Skull 22 + shoulder 4 + spine 26 + rib cage 24 + pelvis 2
- C) Skull 14 + ear 6 + spine 33 + rib cage 25 + hyoid 1
- D) Skull 22 + ear 6 + hands 54 − pelvis 2
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

The total is 22 skull + 6 ear + 26 adult vertebral-column + 25 rib-cage + 1 hyoid = 80.

**Trap:** B mixes appendicular girdle bones into the list and omits the sternum from the rib-cage count.

**See also:** [[../../biology/21 - Bone#Key concepts]]
</details>

@qbio.bone.q12 difficulty=tricky subject=biology chapter=21
Assertion (A): The adult vertebral column is counted as 26 bones although it develops from more than 26 vertebrae.
Reason (R): Five sacral vertebrae fuse into one sacrum and four coccygeal vertebrae fuse into one coccyx.

- A) Both A and R are true, and R correctly explains A
- B) Both A and R are true, but R does not explain A
- C) A is true, but R is false
- D) A is false, but R is true
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

Adults count 7 cervical + 12 thoracic + 5 lumbar + 1 sacrum + 1 coccyx = 26. Sacral and coccygeal fusion explains the lower adult count.

**Trap:** D adds five sacral and four coccygeal elements separately even when counting the fused adult skeleton.

**See also:** [[../../biology/21 - Bone#Bones in Spinal Cord / Vertebral Column (26 in adults)]]
</details>
