@qbio.tooth.q1 difficulty=recall subject=biology chapter=20
The adult human dental formula for one side of one jaw is:

- A) 2 incisors, 1 canine, 2 premolars, 3 molars
- B) 2 incisors, 1 canine, 0 premolars, 2 molars
- C) 1 incisor, 2 canines, 2 premolars, 3 molars
- D) 2 incisors, 2 canines, 1 premolar, 3 molars
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

The adult formula per quadrant is I 2, C 1, P 2, M 3. Multiplying its eight teeth by four quadrants gives 32 teeth.

**Trap:** B is the milk-tooth formula; children have no premolars.

**See also:** [[../../biology/20 - Tooth#DENTAL FORMULA]]
</details>

@qbio.tooth.q2 difficulty=recall subject=biology chapter=20
Which part of a tooth is the hardest substance in the human body?

- A) Dentin
- B) Cement
- C) Enamel
- D) Pulp
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C**

Enamel is the hard, calcium-phosphate-rich outer covering of the crown and is the hardest substance in the human body.

**Trap:** Dentin forms most of the tooth, but being the largest layer does not make it the hardest.

**See also:** [[../../biology/20 - Tooth#Tooth Anatomy (parts)]]
</details>

@qbio.tooth.q3 difficulty=recall subject=biology chapter=20
Nerves and blood vessels of a tooth are principally found in the:

- A) Enamel
- B) Pulp cavity
- C) Cement
- D) Crown surface
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

The pulp cavity is the innermost soft region containing nerves and blood vessels. The root canal carries this pulp through the root.

**Trap:** Enamel is hard, mineralised outer material and contains neither the tooth's nerves nor its blood vessels.

**See also:** [[../../biology/20 - Tooth#Tooth Anatomy (parts)]]
</details>

@qbio.tooth.q4 difficulty=apply subject=biology chapter=20
A dentist finds decay in the hard tissue lying immediately beneath the enamel and forming most of the tooth. Which tissue is affected?

- A) Dentin
- B) Gingiva
- C) Pulp
- D) Cement
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

Dentin lies beneath the enamel and forms the bulk of a tooth.

**Trap:** Cement is also bone-like, but it covers the root rather than making up most of the tooth beneath crown enamel.

**See also:** [[../../biology/20 - Tooth#Tooth Anatomy (parts)]]
</details>

@qbio.tooth.q5 difficulty=apply subject=biology chapter=20
A child has a complete set of milk teeth. How many teeth and premolars should be present?

- A) 20 teeth, including 4 premolars
- B) 20 teeth, with no premolars
- C) 24 teeth, with no molars
- D) 32 teeth, including 8 premolars
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

The child dental formula is 2, 1, 0, 2 per quadrant, giving 20 teeth in total and no premolars.

**Trap:** A remembers the total of 20 but imports premolars from adult dentition.

**See also:** [[../../biology/20 - Tooth#DENTAL FORMULA]]
</details>

@qbio.tooth.q6 difficulty=apply subject=biology chapter=20
A tooth specialised for tearing food is damaged. Which type is it most likely to be?

- A) Incisor
- B) Canine
- C) Premolar
- D) Molar
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

Canines are adapted for tearing. Incisors bite and cut, premolars grind, and molars chew and crush.

**Trap:** Incisors are prominent front teeth and may seem suited to tearing, but their listed primary function is biting and cutting.

**See also:** [[../../biology/20 - Tooth#TOOTH — TYPES AND COUNT]]
</details>

@qbio.tooth.q7 difficulty=apply subject=biology chapter=20
Damage to the bone-like covering of a tooth's root would most directly weaken its anchorage to the jaw. Which layer is damaged?

- A) Enamel
- B) Dentin
- C) Cement
- D) Gingiva
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C**

Cement covers the root and helps anchor the tooth to the jaw. Enamel covers the crown instead.

**Trap:** Gingiva surrounds the tooth, but it is soft gum tissue rather than the bone-like root covering named in the stem.

**See also:** [[../../biology/20 - Tooth#Tooth Anatomy (parts)]]
</details>

@qbio.tooth.q8 difficulty=tricky subject=biology chapter=20
An adult has lost one incisor and one molar from every quadrant. How many teeth remain?

- A) 20
- B) 24
- C) 26
- D) 28
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

A normal adult has 32 teeth. Losing two teeth in each of four quadrants removes 8, so 32 − 8 = 24.

**Trap:** D subtracts only four teeth, overlooking that both an incisor and a molar are lost in each quadrant.

**See also:** [[../../biology/20 - Tooth#Total Adult Teeth: 32]]
</details>

@qbio.tooth.q9 difficulty=tricky subject=biology chapter=20
Consider these statements about one quadrant of adult dentition:
1. Incisors and canines together number three.
2. Premolars and molars together number five.
3. Therefore, one quadrant contains eight teeth.

Which option is correct?

- A) Only statement 1 is correct
- B) Only statements 1 and 2 are correct
- C) All three statements are correct
- D) Only statement 3 is correct
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C**

The adult formula is 2 incisors + 1 canine + 2 premolars + 3 molars. Thus 3 + 5 = 8 teeth per quadrant.

**Trap:** B accepts the component counts but fails to carry out their sum.

**See also:** [[../../biology/20 - Tooth#DENTAL FORMULA]]
</details>

@qbio.tooth.q10 difficulty=tricky subject=biology chapter=20
Which anatomical route correctly proceeds from the outside of the crown toward the tooth's soft neurovascular centre?

- A) Enamel → dentin → pulp cavity
- B) Cement → enamel → pulp cavity
- C) Pulp cavity → dentin → enamel
- D) Gingiva → cement → crown
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

At the crown, enamel is outermost, dentin lies beneath it, and the pulp cavity is the innermost soft region containing nerves and vessels.

**Trap:** B begins with cement, which covers the root rather than the crown.

**See also:** [[../../biology/20 - Tooth#Tooth Anatomy (parts)]]
</details>
