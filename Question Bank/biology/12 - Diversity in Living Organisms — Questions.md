@qbio.diversity.q1 difficulty=recall subject=biology chapter=12
The correct descending order of taxonomic ranks is:

- A) Species → Genus → Family → Order → Class → Phylum → Kingdom
- B) Kingdom → Phylum → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species
- C) Kingdom → Class → Phylum → Order → Family → Genus → Species
- D) Phylum → Kingdom → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

Standard hierarchy (highest → lowest): **Kingdom → Phylum → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species**. Mnemonic: *King Philip Came Over For Good Spaghetti*.

**Trap:** C and D scramble Phylum and Class (or Kingdom and Phylum). Stick to the mnemonic.

**See also:** [[../../biology/12 - Diversity in Living Organisms#Taxonomic Hierarchy (7 mandatory ranks)]]
</details>

@qbio.diversity.q2 difficulty=recall subject=biology chapter=12
The **Five Kingdom Classification** was proposed by:

- A) Carolus Linnaeus
- B) Ernst Haeckel
- C) R. H. Whittaker
- D) Carl Woese
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C**

**R. H. Whittaker** (1969) proposed the Five Kingdom Classification: Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, Animalia. Linnaeus proposed Two Kingdom (1758); Haeckel proposed Three Kingdom (1866); Woese proposed Three Domain (1990).

**Trap:** A (Linnaeus) is the Father of Taxonomy but proposed only Two Kingdom. D (Woese) proposed *Three Domain*, not Five Kingdom.

**See also:** [[../../biology/12 - Diversity in Living Organisms#Five Kingdom Classification (1969)]]
</details>

@qbio.diversity.q3 difficulty=recall subject=biology chapter=12
Which kingdom contains **prokaryotic** organisms (no true nucleus) such as bacteria and cyanobacteria?

- A) Protista
- B) Monera
- C) Fungi
- D) Plantae
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

**Monera** = prokaryotes, including bacteria and cyanobacteria (blue-green algae). Protista = eukaryotic unicellular. Fungi = eukaryotic heterotrophic spore-producers. Plantae = eukaryotic autotrophic multicellular.

**Trap:** A (Protista) is also unicellular but eukaryotic (has a true nucleus).

**See also:** [[../../biology/12 - Diversity in Living Organisms#Five Kingdom Classification (1969)]]
</details>

@qbio.diversity.q4 difficulty=apply subject=biology chapter=12
A new organism is discovered. It is unicellular, has a true nucleus, lives in pond water, and engulfs food particles. It cannot photosynthesise. The kingdom is:

- A) Monera
- B) Protista
- C) Fungi
- D) Plantae
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

**Protista** = eukaryotic unicellular organisms (e.g. *Amoeba*, *Paramecium*, *Euglena*). The clues rule out Monera (prokaryotic — no true nucleus), Fungi (multicellular, saprophytic/parasitic), and Plantae (autotrophic).

**Trap:** A (Monera) is the trap for students who only remember "single-celled" but forget the prokaryote vs eukaryote distinction.

**See also:** [[../../biology/12 - Diversity in Living Organisms#Three Kingdom Classification (1866)]]
</details>

@qbio.diversity.q5 difficulty=recall subject=biology chapter=12
The **Three Domain Classification** (Bacteria, Archaea, Eukarya) was proposed by:

- A) Ernst Haeckel
- B) R. H. Whittaker
- C) Carl Woese
- D) Carolus Linnaeus
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C**

**Carl Woese** (1990) proposed the Three Domain system based on ribosomal RNA differences: **Bacteria**, **Archaea** (extremophiles like *Halobacterium*), **Eukarya** (all eukaryotes).

**Trap:** B (Whittaker) is the Five Kingdom proposer; A (Haeckel) proposed Three Kingdom. The "Domain" concept is specifically Woese's contribution.

**See also:** [[../../biology/12 - Diversity in Living Organisms#Three Domain Classification (1990)]]
</details>

@qbio.diversity.q6 difficulty=apply subject=biology chapter=12
The classification of an organism can be based on which of the following criteria?

- A) Cell nature (prokaryote/eukaryote) only
- B) Mode of nutrition only
- C) Cell nature, cell occurrence, mode of nutrition, and level of body organisation
- D) Only the body shape
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C**

The chapter identifies four classification axes:
- **Cell nature**: Prokaryote / Eukaryote
- **Cell occurrence**: Unicellular / Multicellular
- **Mode of nutrition**: Autotroph / Heterotroph
- **Body organisation level**: Cellular / Tissue / Organ / Organ system

**Trap:** A, B, and D each name only *one* axis — too narrow.

**See also:** [[../../biology/12 - Diversity in Living Organisms#Types of Classification Axes]]
</details>

@qbio.diversity.q7 difficulty=tricky subject=biology chapter=12
Match each scientist with their classification system:

| Scientist | System |
|---|---|
| (i) Linnaeus | (a) Three Domain |
| (ii) Haeckel | (b) Five Kingdom |
| (iii) Whittaker | (c) Two Kingdom |
| (iv) Woese | (d) Three Kingdom |

- A) i-c, ii-d, iii-b, iv-a
- B) i-d, ii-c, iii-b, iv-a
- C) i-c, ii-b, iii-d, iv-a
- D) i-a, ii-b, iii-c, iv-d
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

- (i) **Linnaeus** → Two Kingdom (1758) = (c)
- (ii) **Haeckel** → Three Kingdom (1866, added Protista) = (d)
- (iii) **Whittaker** → Five Kingdom (1969) = (b)
- (iv) **Woese** → Three Domain (1990) = (a)

**Trap:** C swaps Whittaker and Woese; D is fully scrambled.

**See also:** [[../../biology/12 - Diversity in Living Organisms#Types of Classification Systems]]
</details>

@qbio.diversity.q8 difficulty=apply subject=biology chapter=12
The mnemonic **"Some Girls Of Our Class Play Kabaddi"** corresponds to which taxonomic ranks (top-down)?

- A) Species, Genus, Family, Order, Class, Phylum, Kingdom
- B) Species, Genus, Order, Family, Class, Phylum, Kingdom
- C) Kingdom, Phylum, Class, Order, Family, Genus, Species
- D) Kingdom, Class, Order, Family, Phylum, Genus, Species
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

The mnemonic maps (top-down): **Species → Genus → Family → Order → Class → Phylum → Kingdom**. Note: this mnemonic is *descending* — start from the most specific (species) and go up to kingdom.

**Trap:** C is the *correct order* but reversed (lowest to highest), which would correspond to a different mnemonic (Kingdom... Species).

**See also:** [[../../biology/12 - Diversity in Living Organisms#Taxonomic Hierarchy (7 mandatory ranks)]]
</details>

@qbio.diversity.q9 difficulty=recall subject=biology chapter=12
**Autotrophs** differ from heterotrophs in that autotrophs:

- A) Cannot make their own food
- B) Make their own food from inorganic substances (e.g. via photosynthesis)
- C) Only consume other animals
- D) Are always unicellular
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

**Autotrophs** make their own food from inorganic substances — green plants via photosynthesis, some bacteria via chemosynthesis. **Heterotrophs** (animals, fungi) must obtain food from other organisms.

**Trap:** A is the opposite of autotrophy. C describes one type of heterotroph (carnivore), not autotroph. D is unrelated — autotrophs can be unicellular (cyanobacteria) or multicellular (trees).

**See also:** [[../../biology/12 - Diversity in Living Organisms#Types of Classification Axes]]
</details>

@qbio.diversity.q10 difficulty=tricky subject=biology chapter=12
Consider the following statements about classification:
1. Taxonomy is the science of describing, naming, and classifying organisms.
2. Linnaeus published *Systema Naturae* in 1735.
3. The Five Kingdom Classification is based on cell structure, mode of nutrition, body organisation, and reproduction.

Which is correct?

- A) Only statements 1 and 2 are true
- B) Only statements 1 and 3 are true
- C) Only statements 2 and 3 are true
- D) All three statements are true
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

All three are true:
- Statement 1 ✓ — Taxonomy = science of describing, naming, classifying organisms.
- Statement 2 ✓ — Linnaeus published *Systema Naturae* in 1735 (and proposed Two Kingdom Classification in 1758).
- Statement 3 ✓ — Whittaker's Five Kingdom Classification is based on cell structure, mode of nutrition, body organisation, and reproduction.

**Trap:** Statement 3 is sometimes overlooked because students remember only the 5 kingdom *names*, not the *basis* of classification.

**See also:** [[../../biology/12 - Diversity in Living Organisms#Taxonomy]]
</details>
