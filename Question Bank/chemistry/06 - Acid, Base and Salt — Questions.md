@qchem.acid.q1 difficulty=recall subject=chemistry chapter=6
How does an acid affect blue litmus paper?

- A) It turns it red
- B) It turns it blue
- C) It turns it pink
- D) It causes no stated change
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

An acid turns blue litmus paper red; a base turns red litmus paper blue.

**See also:** [[../../chemistry/06 - Acid, Base and Salt#Acid]]
</details>

@qchem.acid.q5 difficulty=apply subject=chemistry chapter=6
A gas produced by an acid-carbonate reaction turns lime water milky. What causes the milkiness?

- A) Soluble sodium chloride forms
- B) Insoluble calcium carbonate forms
- C) Hydrogen burns with a pop
- D) Calcium hydroxide evaporates
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

Carbon dioxide reacts with calcium hydroxide in lime water to form insoluble white calcium carbonate, causing the milky appearance.

**See also:** [[../../chemistry/06 - Acid, Base and Salt#Test of Carbon Dioxide]]
</details>

@qchem.acid.q6 difficulty=apply subject=chemistry chapter=6
Which is the safe method for diluting concentrated acid?

- A) Add water rapidly to acid
- B) Add acid slowly to water with constant stirring
- C) Mix equal volumes without stirring
- D) Heat the acid before adding water
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

Acid should be added slowly to water with constant stirring. Adding water to acid can produce intense exothermic heating, splashing, and burns.

**See also:** [[../../chemistry/06 - Acid, Base and Salt#Dilution of Acids]]
</details>

@qchem.acid.q8 difficulty=apply subject=chemistry chapter=6
Which products are formed when aqueous brine undergoes the chlor-alkali process?

- A) NaOH, Cl₂, and H₂
- B) NaHCO₃ and NH₄Cl
- C) CaOCl₂ and H₂O only
- D) Na₂CO₃ and CO₂ only
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: A**

Electrolysis of aqueous sodium chloride produces sodium hydroxide, chlorine, and hydrogen: `2NaCl + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + Cl₂ + H₂`.

**See also:** [[../../chemistry/06 - Acid, Base and Salt#Chlor-Alkali Process]]
</details>

A salt is formed from a weak acid and a strong base. How does the chapter classify that salt?

@qchem.acid.q11 difficulty=tricky subject=chemistry chapter=6
Consider these statements:

1. Acid rain has pH below 5.6.
2. Tooth decay starts below pH 5.5.
3. Blood at pH 7.4 is slightly alkaline.

Which option is correct?

- A) Only 1 and 2
- B) Only 2 and 3
- C) Only 1 and 3
- D) 1, 2, and 3
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

All three values and interpretations are stated: acid rain below 5.6, tooth decay below 5.5, and blood at pH 7.4 as slightly alkaline.

**See also:** [[../../chemistry/06 - Acid, Base and Salt#Importance of pH Scale in Everyday Life]]
</details>

@qchem.acid.q12 difficulty=tricky subject=chemistry chapter=6
Plaster of Paris is left in a humid, open container. What change explains why moisture-proof storage is required?

- A) It loses all calcium and becomes CO₂
- B) It absorbs moisture and forms hard gypsum
- C) It changes into washing soda
- D) It releases chlorine and forms bleaching powder
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

Plaster of Paris absorbs moisture and changes to hard gypsum. Therefore it is always stored in a moisture-proof container.

**See also:** [[../../chemistry/06 - Acid, Base and Salt#Note]]
</details>
