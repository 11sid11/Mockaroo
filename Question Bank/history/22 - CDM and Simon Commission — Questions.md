@qhis.cdm_and_simon_commission.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=22
Which statement about CDM and Simon Commission is correct?

- A) Lala Lajpat Rai was injured during a protest against Simon Commission and later died
- B) The Civil Disobedience Movement began with Gandhi’s Salt March from Sabarmati to Dandi in 1930
- C) The Gandhi-Irwin Pact was signed in 1931 before the Second Round Table Conference
- D) The Simon Commission arrived in India in 1928 without any Indian member
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

The Simon Commission arrived in India in 1928 without any Indian member

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.
