> Source: [[../../history/06 - Maurya Dynasty.md|Maurya Dynasty]]

@qhis.maurya_dynasty.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=6
Which statement about Maurya Dynasty is correct?

- A) Megasthenes was sent as ambassador to Chandragupta Maurya’s court at Pataliputra
- B) Ashoka fought the Kalinga War in 261 BCE and replaced Bherighosha with Dhammaghosha
- C) James Prinsep was the first to decipher Ashokan inscriptions in 1837
- D) Chandragupta Maurya founded the Mauryan Dynasty in 322 BCE with Kautilya
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

Chandragupta Maurya founded the Mauryan Dynasty in 322 BCE with Kautilya

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.

**See also:** [[../../history/06 - Maurya Dynasty.md#Content|Maurya Dynasty — source chapter]]
</details>
