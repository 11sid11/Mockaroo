@qhis.revolt_of_1857.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=19
Which statement about Revolt of 1857 is correct?

- A) Mangal Pandey attacked British officers at Barrackpore on 29 March 1857
- B) Rani Lakshmibai led the revolt at Jhansi while Kunwar Singh led it in Bihar
- C) The Government of India Act 1858 ended Company rule and made the Governor-General the Viceroy
- D) The Revolt of 1857 began at Meerut on 10 May 1857
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

The Revolt of 1857 began at Meerut on 10 May 1857

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.

**See also:** [[../../history/19 - Revolt of 1857.md#Content|Revolt of 1857 — source chapter]]
