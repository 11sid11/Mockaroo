@qhis.gandhian_era.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=21
Which statement about Gandhian Era is correct?

- A) Champaran Satyagraha (1917) opposed the Tinkathia system of indigo cultivation
- B) The Non-Cooperation Movement was withdrawn after the Chauri Chaura incident in 1922
- C) The Swaraj Party was formed in 1923 by C. R. Das and Motilal Nehru
- D) Gandhi returned to India on 9 January 1915 and Gopal Krishna Gokhale was his political guru
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

Gandhi returned to India on 9 January 1915 and Gopal Krishna Gokhale was his political guru

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.

</details>
