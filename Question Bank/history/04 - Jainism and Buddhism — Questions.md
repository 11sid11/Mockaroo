@qhis.jainism_and_buddhism.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=4
Which statement about Jainism and Buddhism is correct?

- A) The Four Noble Truths identify desire as the cause of suffering and the Eightfold Path as the way to end it
- B) The Third Buddhist Council was held at Pataliputra under Ashoka and presided over by Moggaliputta Tissa
- C) The Fourth Buddhist Council under Kanishka at Kashmir led to the division into Mahayana and Hinayana
- D) Mahavira was the 24th Tirthankara and was born at Kundagram near Vaishali
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

Mahavira was the 24th Tirthankara and was born at Kundagram near Vaishali

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.

**See also:** [[../../history/04 - Jainism and Buddhism.md#Content|Jainism and Buddhism — source chapter]]
</details>
