> Source: [[../../history/01 - Stone Age.md|Stone Age]]

@qhis.stone_age.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=1
Which statement about Stone Age is correct?

- A) Bhimbhetka rock caves were discovered by V. S. Wakankar in 1957
- B) Mesolithic humans were hunters and herders and domestication of animals began in this period
- C) Flake technology involved striking a stone core to remove flakes
- D) Palaeolithic means Old Stone Age and preceded the Mesolithic
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

Palaeolithic means Old Stone Age and preceded the Mesolithic

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.

**See also:** [[../../history/01 - Stone Age.md#Content|Stone Age — source chapter]]
</details>
