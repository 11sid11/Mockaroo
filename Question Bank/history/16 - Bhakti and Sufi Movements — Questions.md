@qhis.bhakti_and_sufi_movements.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=16
Which statement about Bhakti and Sufi Movements is correct?

- A) Ramanuja propounded Vishishtadvaita while Madhva propounded Dvaita
- B) Guru Angad introduced the Gurmukhi script
- C) Guru Gobind Singh established the Khalsa Panth on Vaisakhi in 1699
- D) Kabir belonged to the Nirguna sect and Bijaka compiles his preachings
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

Kabir belonged to the Nirguna sect and Bijaka compiles his preachings

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.
