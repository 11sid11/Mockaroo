@qhis.socio_religious_reforms.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=18
Which statement about Socio-Religious Reforms is correct?

- A) Sir Syed Ahmad Khan started the Aligarh Movement and the Mohammedan Anglo Oriental College
- B) Jyotiba Phule founded Satyashodhak Samaj in 1873 against caste discrimination
- C) Swami Vivekananda founded Ramakrishna Mission in 1897 with headquarters at Belur Math
- D) Swami Dayanand Saraswati founded Arya Samaj in 1875 and wrote Satyarth Prakash
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

Swami Dayanand Saraswati founded Arya Samaj in 1875 and wrote Satyarth Prakash

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.
