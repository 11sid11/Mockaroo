@qhis.mughal_empire.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=14
Which statement about Mughal Empire is correct?

- A) Sher Shah Suri introduced the Rupia and gave peasants Patta and Qabuliyat
- B) Akbar abolished the Jizya in 1564 and introduced the Mansabdari system
- C) Aurangzeb reimposed Jizya in 1679 and was the last major Mughal emperor
- D) Babur founded the Mughal Dynasty after defeating Ibrahim Lodi in the First Battle of Panipat in 1526
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

Babur founded the Mughal Dynasty after defeating Ibrahim Lodi in the First Battle of Panipat in 1526

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.

**See also:** [[../../history/14 - Mughal Empire.md#Content|Mughal Empire — source chapter]]
</details>
