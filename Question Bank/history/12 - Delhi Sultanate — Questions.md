@qhis.delhi_sultanate.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=12
Which statement about Delhi Sultanate is correct?

- A) The First Battle of Tarain in 1191 was won by Prithviraj Chauhan and the Second in 1192 by Muhammad Ghori
- B) Qutb-ud-din Aibak was called Lakh Baksh and ruled from Lahore
- C) Ibrahim Lodi was the last ruler of the Delhi Sultanate and lost to Babur at Panipat in 1526
- D) The Delhi Sultanate lasted from 1206 to 1526 AD
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

The Delhi Sultanate lasted from 1206 to 1526 AD

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.

**See also:** [[../../history/12 - Delhi Sultanate.md#Content|Delhi Sultanate — source chapter]]
</details>
