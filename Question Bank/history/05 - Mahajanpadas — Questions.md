@qhis.mahajanpadas.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=5
Which statement about Mahajanpadas is correct?

- A) The capital of Vatsa was Kausambi
- B) Magadha rose due to iron mines, elephants, strategic position and capable rulers
- C) Udayin shifted the capital of Magadha from Rajgriha to Pataliputra
- D) Sixteen Mahajanapadas are mentioned in Anguttara Nikaya
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

Sixteen Mahajanapadas are mentioned in Anguttara Nikaya

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.

**See also:** [[../../history/05 - Mahajanpadas.md#Content|Mahajanpadas — source chapter]]
</details>
