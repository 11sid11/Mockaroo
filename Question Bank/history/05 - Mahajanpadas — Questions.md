@qhis.mahajanpadas.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=5
The Buddhist text Anguttara Nikaya mentions the number of Mahajanapadas as:

- A) 12
- B) 16
- C) 18
- D) 20
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

The Anguttara Nikaya (and the Mahavagga) mention 16 Mahajanapadas — the great states of the 6th century BCE.

**Trap:** A and D are easy guesses because students confuse with ganas/sanghas. C is an arbitrary distractor.

@qhis.mahajanpadas.q3 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=5
The capital of the Magadha Mahajanapada was initially:

- A) Pataliputra
- B) Rajgriha (Rajgir)
- C) Vaishali
- D) Kausambi
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: B**

Magadha's first capital was Rajgriha (Rajgir); it was later shifted to Pataliputra by Udayin / the later rulers.

**Trap:** A is the more famous later capital (Mauryan era). C is the capital of the Vajjian confederacy. D is the capital of Vatsa.

@qhis.mahajanpadas.q9 difficulty=apply subject=history chapter=5
Which of the following statements is INCORRECT about Mahajanapadas?

- A) The earliest reference to the 16 Mahajanapadas is in the Buddhist Anguttara Nikaya
- B) Magadha's first capital was Rajgriha, later shifted to Pataliputra
- C) Kashi was an important Mahajanapada with capital at Kausambi

@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: C**

Kashi (Banaras/Varanasi) had its capital at Kashi, not Kausambi. Kausambi was the capital of Vatsa, a different Mahajanapada.

**Trap:** A and B are correct textbook facts. D is correct. C is the classic capital-mismatch trap.

@qhis.mahajanpadas.q11 difficulty=tricky subject=history chapter=5
Consider the following statements about Magadha:

1. Magadha was bounded by the Ganga, Son, Champa and the Chota Nagpur plateau.
2. The first three important rulers of Magadha were Bimbisara, Ajatashatru and Udayin of the Haryanka dynasty.
3. Magadha had a strong, centralised monarchy with the king at the apex.

Which of the above are correct?

- A) 1 and 2 only
- B) 2 and 3 only
- C) 1 and 3 only
- D) 1, 2 and 3
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

All three statements are correct. Magadha's geography gave it strategic and economic advantages; the Haryanka line included Bimbisara, Ajatashatru and Udayin; and the kingdom was a centralised monarchy unlike the gana-sanghas of Vajjian type.

**Trap:** A and C drop a true statement. B drops the geographic statement which is often forgotten.

**See also:** [[../../history/05 - Mahajanpadas.md#Content|Mahajanpadas — source chapter]]
</details>
