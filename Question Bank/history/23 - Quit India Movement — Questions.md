@qhis.quit_india_movement.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=23
Which statement about Quit India Movement is correct?

- A) Gandhi launched the Quit India Movement on 8 August 1942 with the slogan Do or Die
- B) The movement became leaderless after its leaders were arrested on the first day
- C) Usha Mehta ran an underground radio in Bombay during the movement
- D) The Quit India Resolution was adopted at Wardha in July 1942 and ratified at Bombay
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

The Quit India Resolution was adopted at Wardha in July 1942 and ratified at Bombay

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.
