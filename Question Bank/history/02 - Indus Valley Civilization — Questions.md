@qhis.indus_valley_civilization.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=2
Which statement about Indus Valley Civilization is correct?

- A) Harappan roads intersected at right angles in a grid pattern
- B) Harappan script was pictographic, written from right to left, and remains undeciphered
- C) Chanhudaro was a centre of bead making, shell cutting, metal working, seal making and weights
- D) Harappan seals were made of steatite and often carried animal motifs
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

Harappan seals were made of steatite and often carried animal motifs

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.

**See also:** [[../../history/02 - Indus Valley Civilization.md#Content|Indus Valley Civilization — source chapter]]
</details>
