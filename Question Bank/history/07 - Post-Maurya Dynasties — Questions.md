@qhis.post_maurya_dynasties.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=7
Which statement about Post-Maurya Dynasties is correct?

- A) The Satavahana capital was Paithan or Pratisthan
- B) Menander (Milinda) was an Indo-Greek ruler whose dialogue with Nagasena is Milindapanho
- C) Kanishka started the Saka Era in 78 AD and patronised the Fourth Buddhist Council
- D) Pushyamitra Shunga founded the Shunga Dynasty after killing Brihadratha Maurya
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

Pushyamitra Shunga founded the Shunga Dynasty after killing Brihadratha Maurya

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.

**See also:** [[../../history/07 - Post-Maurya Dynasties.md#Content|Post-Maurya Dynasties — source chapter]]
</details>
