@qhis.marathas.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=15
Which statement about Marathas is correct?

- A) The Treaty of Purandar (1665) required Shivaji to surrender 23 forts to the Mughals
- B) Chauth was one-fourth of land revenue and Sardeshmukhi was an additional ten percent
- C) The Third Battle of Panipat in 1761 was fought between Marathas and Ahmad Shah Abdali
- D) Shivaji was crowned Chhatrapati at Raigad in 1674
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

Shivaji was crowned Chhatrapati at Raigad in 1674

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.

**See also:** [[../../history/15 - Marathas.md#Content|Marathas — source chapter]]
</details>
