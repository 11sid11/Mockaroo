@qhis.post_gupta_dynasties.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=9
Which statement about Post-Gupta Dynasties is correct?

- A) Pulakeshin II defeated Harshavardhana on the banks of the Narmada
- B) The Badami Chalukya capital was Vatapi
- C) Dantidurga founded the Rashtrakuta Dynasty and their capital was Manyakheta
- D) Harshavardhana made Kannauj his capital and ruled from 606 to 647 AD
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

Harshavardhana made Kannauj his capital and ruled from 606 to 647 AD

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.

**See also:** [[../../history/09 - Post-Gupta Dynasties.md#Content|Post-Gupta Dynasties — source chapter]]
</details>
