@qhis.vijayanagara_and_bahmani_kingdom.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=13
Which statement about Vijayanagara and Bahmani Kingdom is correct?

- A) Krishna Deva Raya belonged to the Tuluva Dynasty and ruled from 1509 to 1529
- B) Ala-ud-din Hasan Bahman Shah founded the Bahmani Kingdom with Gulbarga as its first capital
- C) Ahmad Shah Wali shifted the Bahmani capital from Gulbarga to Bidar
- D) Harihara and Bukka founded the Vijayanagara Empire in 1336 and its capital was Hampi
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

Harihara and Bukka founded the Vijayanagara Empire in 1336 and its capital was Hampi
