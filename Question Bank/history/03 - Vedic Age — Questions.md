> Source: [[../../history/03 - Vedic Age.md|Vedic Age]]

@qhis.vedic_age.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=3
Which statement about Vedic Age is correct?

- A) The Rig Veda was the first Veda compiled and has 10 mandalas
- B) The Battle of Ten Kings was fought on the Purushini (Ravi) and was won by Bharata chief Sudas
- C) In the Later Vedic period iron and horses helped expansion from Punjab towards eastern Uttar Pradesh
- D) The Early Vedic Age is dated 1500–1000 BC and was pastoral
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

The Early Vedic Age is dated 1500–1000 BC and was pastoral

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.

**See also:** [[../../history/03 - Vedic Age.md#Content|Vedic Age — source chapter]]
</details>
