@qhis.gupta_dynasty.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=8
Which statement about Gupta Dynasty is correct?

- A) Samudragupta’s achievements are recorded in the Prayag Prasasti composed by Harisena
- B) Chandragupta II took the title Vikramaditya after defeating the Sakas
- C) Kumaragupta I founded Nalanda University
- D) Sri Gupta was the founder of the Gupta Dynasty
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

Sri Gupta was the founder of the Gupta Dynasty

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.

**See also:** [[../../history/08 - Gupta Dynasty.md#Content|Gupta Dynasty — source chapter]]
</details>
