@qhis.advent_of_europeans.q1 difficulty=recall subject=history chapter=17
Which statement about Advent of Europeans is correct?

- A) The Dutch East India Company was formed in 1602 and set up its first factory at Masulipatnam
- B) The English first established a permanent factory at Surat in 1613
- C) The Battle of Plassey in 1757 confirmed the formal beginning of British rule in India
- D) Vasco da Gama reached Calicut in 1498 during the rule of Zamorin
@@

<details><summary>Answer & explanation</summary>

**Answer: D**

Vasco da Gama reached Calicut in 1498 during the rule of Zamorin

**Trap:** The other options are drawn from nearby facts in the same chapter and are easy to confuse when similar rulers, dates or movements are mixed up.

**See also:** [[../../history/17 - Advent of Europeans.md#Content|Advent of Europeans — source chapter]]
</details>
